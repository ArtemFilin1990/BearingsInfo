# Bearing Types Taxonomy

## Purpose
This document provides a comprehensive taxonomy of rolling bearing types with detailed descriptions and applications.

## Complete Bearing Type List

### Ball Bearings (Шариковые подшипники)

#### 1. Deep Groove Ball Bearings (Шариковые радиальные однорядные)

**ГОСТ Series:** 0, 60, 61, 62, 63, 64, 160, 180, 19, 200

**Description:**
Most versatile and widely used bearing type. Deep raceway grooves (~30% of ball diameter) provide good radial and limited axial load capacity.

**Key Features:**
- Single row of balls
- Deep conforming raceways
- Low friction coefficient
- High speed capability
- Can handle axial loads in both directions

**Load Capacity:**
- Radial: Good
- Axial: Moderate (up to 50% of radial load)
- Combined: Yes

**Speed:** Very high (n·dm up to 1,000,000)

**Applications:**
- Electric motors (most common)
- Gearboxes
- Automotive components
- Household appliances
- General machinery

**Variants:**
- Standard (open)
- Single shield (Z)
- Double shield (ZZ, 2Z)
- Single seal (RS, RSL, RSR)
- Double seal (2RS, 2RSL, 2RSR)
- Snap ring groove (N, NR)
- Filling slot (C)

#### 2. Angular Contact Ball Bearings (Шариковые радиально-упорные)

**ГОСТ Series:** 36, 46, 66, 76, 234, 36000, 46000

**Description:**
Designed with contact angle between balls and raceways, enabling high axial load capacity in one direction.

**Key Features:**
- Contact angle: typically 15°, 25°, 40°
- Asymmetric design
- High axial stiffness
- Used in pairs or sets
- High speed capability

**Contact Angles:**
- 15° (C) - primarily radial loads, some axial
- 25° (A) - balanced radial and axial loads  
- 40° (B) - primarily axial loads

**Mounting Arrangements:**
- **Back-to-back (DB)** - high moment capacity, shaft locating
- **Face-to-face (DF)** - less common, housing locating
- **Tandem (DT)** - double axial capacity, same direction
- **Triplex, Quadruplex** - further increased capacity

**Load Capacity:**
- Radial: Good
- Axial: Very high (one direction per bearing)
- Combined: Excellent

**Speed:** Very high (n·dm up to 1,200,000)

**Applications:**
- Machine tool spindles
- Automotive wheel hubs
- Compressors
- Pumps
- Gearbox shafts

#### 3. Self-Aligning Ball Bearings (Шариковые радиальные сферические двухрядные)

**ГОСТ Series:** 1200, 1300, 1400

**Description:**
Two rows of balls running on a spherical outer ring raceway, allowing angular misalignment.

**Key Features:**
- Spherical outer raceway
- Two rows of balls
- Self-aligning capability: 1.5° - 3°
- Common cage for both rows
- Moderate load capacity

**Load Capacity:**
- Radial: Moderate
- Axial: Low
- Combined: Limited

**Speed:** Moderate to high

**Applications:**
- Agricultural equipment
- Wood processing machinery
- Textile machinery
- Conveyors
- Applications with shaft deflection or misalignment

#### 4. Four-Point Contact Ball Bearings (Шариковые радиально-упорные четырёхточечные)

**ГОСТ Series:** QJ type

**Description:**
Single row bearing with specially designed raceways providing four-point contact, enabling axial loads in both directions.

**Key Features:**
- Four contact points per ball
- Separable design
- Axial loads in both directions
- Lower load capacity than angular contact

**Applications:**
- Gearboxes
- Printing machines
- Robotics
- Applications requiring bidirectional axial capacity

### Roller Bearings (Роликовые подшипники)

#### 5. Cylindrical Roller Bearings (Роликовые с короткими цилиндрическими роликами)

**ГОСТ Series:** 2000, 32000, 42000, 3000, 92000, 50000

**Description:**
Rollers with length/diameter ratio ~1:1 to 3:1, providing high radial load capacity with line contact.

**Key Features:**
- High radial load capacity
- Separable design
- Low friction
- Some types allow axial displacement
- Moderate to high speed

**Design Types:**
- **NU** - outer ring with ribs, inner ring without ribs (axially free)
- **N** - inner ring with ribs, outer ring without ribs
- **NJ** - outer ring with ribs, inner ring with one integral rib
- **NUP** - NJ + separate loose rib on inner ring
- **NF** - outer ring with one integral rib + separate loose rib

**Load Capacity:**
- Radial: Very high
- Axial: None (NU, N) to Limited (NJ, NUP)
- Combined: Limited

**Speed:** Moderate to high (n·dm up to 500,000)

**Applications:**
- Gearboxes
- Electric motors (large)
- Railway axleboxes
- Rolling mills
- Paper machines

#### 6. Needle Roller Bearings (Роликовые игольчатые)

**ГОСТ Series:** 4000, 5000, RNA, NK, NKI, NKIS

**Description:**
Very small diameter rollers (length/diameter > 2.5), providing high load capacity in minimal radial space.

**Key Features:**
- Very compact cross-section
- High radial load capacity
- Low speed capability
- Require precise alignment
- Often without inner ring

**Types:**
- Drawn cup (HK, BK)
- Machined ring (RNA, NK)
- Needle roller and cage assemblies (K)
- Thrust needle bearings

**Load Capacity:**
- Radial: Very high for size
- Axial: None
- Combined: No

**Speed:** Low to moderate

**Applications:**
- Connecting rods
- Universal joints
- Rocker arms
- Planetary gears
- Compact gearboxes

#### 7. Tapered Roller Bearings (Роликовые конические)

**ГОСТ Series:** 7000, 27000, 97000, 2007000, 30000

**Description:**
Conical rollers running on tapered raceways, with all contact lines converging at a common apex on the bearing axis.

**Key Features:**
- High radial and axial load capacity
- Separable (cup and cone)
- Adjustable for preload or clearance
- Must be mounted in pairs or groups
- Moderate speed

**Configurations:**
- **Single row** - one direction axial load
- **Double row** - two rows, bidirectional axial
- **Four row** - very heavy loads (rolling mills)

**Load Capacity:**
- Radial: Very high
- Axial: Very high (one direction per row)
- Combined: Excellent

**Speed:** Moderate (n·dm up to 300,000)

**Applications:**
- Automotive wheel hubs
- Gearboxes
- Railway axles
- Rolling mills
- Crushers

#### 8. Spherical Roller Bearings (Роликовые сферические двухрядные)

**ГОСТ Series:** 3500, 3600, 53000, 113000, 239000

**Description:**
Two rows of barrel-shaped rollers running on a common spherical outer ring raceway, providing very high load capacity with self-aligning capability.

**Key Features:**
- Self-aligning: 1° - 2.5°
- Very high load capacity
- Barrel-shaped rollers
- Spherical outer raceway
- Symmetric or asymmetric roller design

**Roller Designs:**
- **Symmetric** - older design, point contact in center
- **Asymmetric** - modern design, optimized contact, higher capacity

**Load Capacity:**
- Radial: Extremely high
- Axial: Moderate to high
- Combined: Excellent

**Speed:** Moderate to low (n·dm up to 200,000)

**Applications:**
- Crushers and mills
- Paper machines
- Mining equipment
- Marine propulsion
- Heavy industrial machinery

#### 9. Toroidal Roller Bearings (CARB®)

**Description:**
Advanced spherical roller bearing design with toroidal (hourglass) shaped rollers.

**Key Features:**
- Lower friction than conventional spherical rollers
- Reduced heat generation
- Longer service life
- Self-aligning capability

**Applications:**
- Wind turbines
- Vibrating screens
- Material handling

### Thrust Bearings (Упорные подшипники)

#### 10. Thrust Ball Bearings (Шариковые упорные)

**ГОСТ Series:** 8000, 9000, 5100

**Description:**
Balls running between two washers (raceways) for pure axial load support.

**Key Features:**
- Pure axial load capacity
- Minimal radial load capability
- High speed capability
- Require precise alignment
- Single or double direction

**Types:**
- **Single direction** - one shaft washer + one housing washer
- **Double direction** - two shaft washers + one center housing washer

**Load Capacity:**
- Radial: Minimal (not recommended)
- Axial: Moderate
- Combined: No

**Speed:** High

**Applications:**
- Crane hooks
- Vertical shaft machinery
- Automotive clutches
- Light axial thrust loads

#### 11. Cylindrical Roller Thrust Bearings (Роликовые упорные с цилиндрическими роликами)

**ГОСТ Series:** 81100, 81200, 812, 893

**Description:**
Cylindrical rollers running axially between thrust washers.

**Key Features:**
- Very high axial load capacity
- No radial load capacity
- Lower speed than thrust ball bearings
- Separable design
- Require precise perpendicularity

**Load Capacity:**
- Radial: None
- Axial: Very high
- Combined: No

**Speed:** Moderate

**Applications:**
- Screw presses
- Vertical pumps
- Crane turntables
- Marine propeller shafts

#### 12. Spherical Roller Thrust Bearings (Роликовые упорные сферические)

**ГОСТ Series:** 29000, 294

**Description:**
Asymmetric barrel-shaped rollers running on a spherical raceway, combining very high axial capacity with self-aligning capability.

**Key Features:**
- Very high axial load capacity
- Can support some radial load
- Self-aligning capability
- Asymmetric roller design
- Separable

**Load Capacity:**
- Radial: Moderate
- Axial: Extremely high
- Combined: Yes (some radial)

**Speed:** Low to moderate

**Applications:**
- Hydro turbines
- Marine propeller shafts
- Large vertical motors
- Rolling mills (backup rolls)
- Heavy combined thrust and radial loads

#### 13. Needle Roller Thrust Bearings (Роликовые упорные игольчатые)

**ГОСТ Series:** AXK, NTA

**Description:**
Needle rollers running axially for compact thrust bearing arrangements.

**Key Features:**
- Very compact axial space
- High axial load capacity for size
- Often without cage
- Low speed

**Applications:**
- Automotive transmissions
- Compact gearboxes
- Clutches

### Special and Combined Designs

#### 14. Four-Row Cylindrical Roller Bearings (Роликовые четырёхрядные)

**Description:**
Four rows of cylindrical rollers for extremely high radial loads in rolling mills.

**Applications:**
- Rolling mill backup rolls
- Extremely heavy radial loads

#### 15. Crossed Roller Bearings (Роликовые перекрёстные)

**Description:**
Cylindrical rollers arranged at 90° to each other in a single row, providing radial, axial, and moment load capacity.

**Applications:**
- Robotics
- Machine tool rotary tables
- Medical equipment

#### 16. Linear Bearings (Линейные подшипники)

**Description:**
Bearings designed for linear motion rather than rotary motion.

**Types:**
- Linear ball bearings
- Linear roller bearings  
- Linear guides

**Applications:**
- CNC machines
- Automation equipment
- Precision positioning systems

# Rolling Elements - Balls and Rollers

## Purpose
This document describes the characteristics, materials, manufacturing, and quality requirements for balls and rollers used in rolling bearings.

## Balls (Шарики)

### Characteristics

**Geometry:**
- Perfect spherical shape
- Point contact with raceways
- Diameter range: 0.8 mm to 200 mm (standard)
- Precision diameters available

**Contact Characteristics:**
- **Hertzian point contact** - very small contact area
- Low friction coefficient
- Good for high speeds
- Lower load capacity than line contact (rollers)

### Ball Materials

#### Through-Hardened Bearing Steel

**ГОСТ 801-78: ШХ15**
- Composition: ~1% C, ~1.5% Cr
- Hardness: 60-66 HRC
- Most common ball material
- Excellent wear resistance
- Cost-effective

**International Equivalents:**
- 100Cr6 (Europe)
- SUJ2 (Japan)
- 52100 (USA)
- GCr15 (China)

**Properties:**
- Compressive strength: Very high
- Hardness: 60-66 HRC
- Operating temperature: -40°C to +120°C (standard lubrication)
- Density: 7.81 g/cm³

#### Stainless Steel Balls

**AISI 440C (ГОСТ: 95Х18)**
- Composition: ~1% C, ~17% Cr
- Hardness: 58-60 HRC
- Corrosion resistant
- Slightly lower load capacity than ШХ15
- Higher cost

**Applications:**
- Food processing equipment
- Marine environments
- Medical instruments
- Chemical industry

**Properties:**
- Corrosion resistance: Excellent
- Hardness: 58-60 HRC  
- Operating temperature: -40°C to +150°C
- Density: 7.75 g/cm³

#### Ceramic Balls (Silicon Nitride - Si₃N₄)

**Properties:**
- Very low density: 3.20 g/cm³ (60% lighter than steel)
- Very high hardness: 1400-1800 HV
- Low thermal expansion
- Electrical insulation
- Corrosion resistance
- Operating temperature: -273°C to +800°C (with proper lubrication)

**Advantages:**
- Higher speed capability
- Lower centrifugal forces
- Reduced friction
- No electrical pitting
- Longer life in contaminated conditions

**Disadvantages:**
- Higher cost (10-20x steel balls)
- Brittle - sensitive to impact
- Require special mounting procedures

**Applications:**
- High-speed spindles
- Electric motors (hybrid bearings)
- Aerospace applications
- High-temperature environments

#### Other Materials

**Glass Balls:**
- Low-cost applications
- Non-critical loads
- Decorative or low-precision uses

**Chrome Steel:**
- Alternative to ШХ15
- Similar properties

### Ball Manufacturing

#### Process Steps:

1. **Wire Drawing**
   - High-quality bearing steel wire
   - Precise diameter control

2. **Heading (Cold Forming)**
   - Wire cut to length
   - Cold heading to near-spherical shape
   - Slight material excess for subsequent operations

3. **Heat Treatment**
   - Austenitizing: 820-860°C
   - Quenching: Oil or polymer
   - Tempering: 150-180°C
   - Target hardness: 60-66 HRC

4. **Grinding**
   - Rough grinding to near-final size
   - Fine grinding for precision
   - Between rotating grinding discs

5. **Lapping**
   - Fine surface finish
   - Sphericity refinement
   - Multiple stages with finer abrasive
   - Mirror-like surface finish (Ra < 0.02 μm)

6. **Inspection and Grading**
   - Dimensional sorting
   - Surface quality inspection
   - Hardness verification

### Ball Grades (ISO 3290)

**Grade System:**
Grades indicate dimensional and surface quality precision.

| Grade | Diameter Tolerance | Sphericity | Surface Roughness | Application |
|-------|-------------------|------------|-------------------|-------------|
| G3 | ±0.13 μm | 0.13 μm | Ra ≤ 0.010 μm | Ultra-precision |
| G5 | ±0.25 μm | 0.25 μm | Ra ≤ 0.014 μm | Precision instruments |
| G10 | ±0.5 μm | 0.5 μm | Ra ≤ 0.020 μm | Precision bearings |
| G16 | ±0.8 μm | 0.8 μm | Ra ≤ 0.025 μm | High-quality bearings |
| G20 | ±1.0 μm | 1.0 μm | Ra ≤ 0.032 μm | Standard precision |
| G24 | ±1.2 μm | 1.2 μm | Ra ≤ 0.040 μm | General purpose |
| G28 | ±1.4 μm | 1.4 μm | Ra ≤ 0.050 μm | Low precision |

**Standard bearing balls:** G10 to G20
**Precision bearings:** G5 to G10
**Ultra-precision:** G3

### Ball Set Matching

**Diameter Variation in a Set:**
- All balls in a bearing must be within a very tight diameter range
- Typical variation: ±0.5 μm to ±2 μm depending on bearing precision class
- Unequal ball diameters cause uneven load distribution and vibration

**Sorting:**
- Balls are measured and sorted into diameter groups
- Typical group interval: 1 μm or less
- Each bearing uses balls from a single diameter group

## Rollers (Ролики)

### Cylindrical Rollers (Цилиндрические ролики)

**Geometry:**
- Straight cylindrical shape
- Line contact with raceways
- Length/diameter ratio: typically 1:1 to 3:1
- Diameter range: 2 mm to 100 mm

**Profile:**
- **Straight** - simple, lower cost
- **Crowned (modified)** - slightly convex, reduces edge stress
- **Logarithmic profile** - optimized stress distribution

**Contact:**
- Hertzian line contact
- Much larger contact area than balls
- Higher load capacity
- Higher friction than balls

**Applications:**
- Cylindrical roller bearings
- Moderate to high loads
- High speeds (for rollers)

### Tapered Rollers (Конические ролики)

**Geometry:**
- Conical (tapered) shape
- All contact lines converge at bearing axis apex
- Length typically 1.5x to 3x mean diameter
- Steep angle (cup angle) typically 10° to 30°

**Design Principle:**
- Pure rolling motion (no sliding)
- Apex of cone coincides with bearing axis intersection
- Large rib for roller guidance

**Contact:**
- Line contact on tapered raceway
- Large end guided by rib
- Optimized for combined loads

**Applications:**
- Tapered roller bearings
- Automotive wheel hubs
- Gearboxes
- Heavy machinery

### Spherical Rollers (Сферические ролики)

**Geometry:**
- Barrel-shaped (convex) profile
- Symmetric or asymmetric design
- Length/diameter ratio: ~1:1 to 2:1

**Modern Design - Asymmetric:**
- One side more curved than the other
- Optimized contact stress distribution
- Higher load capacity
- Lower friction

**Contact:**
- Line contact on spherical raceway
- Self-aligning capability
- Large contact area

**Applications:**
- Spherical roller bearings
- Very high loads
- Misalignment expected

### Needle Rollers (Игольчатые ролики)

**Geometry:**
- Very small diameter relative to length
- Length/diameter ratio > 2.5 (typically 3:1 to 10:1)
- Diameter range: 1 mm to 10 mm
- Length: up to 100 mm

**Characteristics:**
- Compact radial section
- High load capacity for size
- Require precise guidance
- Lower speed capability

**End Geometry:**
- **Flat ends** - simple, lower cost
- **Crowned ends** - reduced edge stress

**Applications:**
- Needle roller bearings
- Very limited radial space
- Universal joints
- Connecting rods

### Roller Materials

**Primary Material: Through-Hardened Bearing Steel**
- ГОСТ: ШХ15, ШХ15СГ
- International: 100Cr6, SUJ2, 52100
- Hardness: 58-64 HRC
- Same as balls, but different heat treatment for rollers

**For Large Rollers: Case-Hardened Steel**
- Core: 18ХГТ, 20Х2Н4А
- Surface hardness: 58-63 HRC
- Core hardness: 30-45 HRC
- Tough core with hard surface
- Better impact resistance

### Roller Manufacturing

#### Process Steps:

1. **Bar Stock or Wire**
   - High-quality bearing steel
   - Precise diameter

2. **Cutting to Length**
   - Precision cutting
   - Deburring

3. **Turning (for larger rollers)**
   - Rough machining
   - Near-net shape

4. **Heat Treatment**
   - Through-hardening or case-hardening
   - Quenching and tempering
   - Controlled atmosphere

5. **Centerless Grinding**
   - Precise diameter
   - Cylindrical form
   - Surface finish

6. **Profile Grinding**
   - For crowned or tapered rollers
   - Precise geometric control

7. **Superfinishing**
   - Mirror surface finish
   - Microfinish: Ra < 0.05 μm

8. **Inspection**
   - Diameter, length, profile
   - Surface quality
   - Hardness

### Roller Tolerances

**Diameter Tolerance:**
- Precision bearings: ±1 to ±2 μm
- Standard bearings: ±2 to ±5 μm

**Length Tolerance:**
- Typically ±5 to ±20 μm depending on size

**Profile Tolerance:**
- Crown height: ±1 to ±3 μm
- Form deviation: < 2 μm

**Surface Finish:**
- Raceway surface: Ra ≤ 0.05 μm
- Ground surface: Ra ≤ 0.10 μm

### Roller Set Requirements

**Diameter Sorting:**
- All rollers in a bearing within tight diameter range
- Typical variation: ±2 μm to ±5 μm
- Ensures even load distribution

**Length Matching:**
- Critical for proper load sharing
- Variation affects contact pressure

## Surface Treatments

### Standard Treatments

**Grinding:**
- Removes heat treatment scale
- Achieves dimensional precision
- Creates base surface

**Superfinishing:**
- Removes grinding marks
- Reduces surface roughness
- Improves fatigue life
- Typical Ra: 0.01 to 0.05 μm

### Special Coatings

**Black Oxide (Blackening):**
- Corrosion protection
- Break-in improvement
- Aesthetic

**Electroless Nickel:**
- Corrosion protection
- Electrical insulation
- Chemical resistance

**PVD Coatings:**
- Titanium nitride (TiN)
- Reduced friction
- Increased hardness
- Longer life

**DLC (Diamond-Like Carbon):**
- Very low friction
- High wear resistance
- Expensive

## Quality Control

### Dimensional Inspection

**Measurements:**
- Diameter (balls, rollers)
- Sphericity (balls)
- Cylindricity (cylindrical rollers)
- Profile (crowned, tapered rollers)
- Length (rollers)
- Surface finish

**Methods:**
- Coordinate measuring machines (CMM)
- Optical comparators
- Laser scanning
- Roundness testers

### Material Verification

**Tests:**
- Hardness testing (Rockwell, Vickers)
- Microstructure examination
- Chemical composition analysis
- Residual austenite measurement

### Non-Destructive Testing

**Magnetic Particle Inspection:**
- Detects surface cracks
- Subsurface defects

**Ultrasonic Testing:**
- Internal defects
- Material homogeneity

**Eddy Current:**
- Surface defects
- Heat treatment verification

### Performance Testing

**Sample Testing:**
- Fatigue life testing
- Wear testing
- Stress testing

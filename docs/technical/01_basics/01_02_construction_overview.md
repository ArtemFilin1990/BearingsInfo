# Bearing Construction Overview

## Purpose
This document provides an overview of rolling bearing construction, main components, and their functions.

## Basic Construction

### Complete Bearing Assembly
A typical rolling bearing consists of:
1. **Inner ring** - with inner raceway
2. **Outer ring** - with outer raceway
3. **Rolling elements** - balls, rollers, or needles
4. **Cage/separator** - to maintain element spacing
5. **Optional: Seals or shields** - for protection and lubrication retention

### Separable vs Non-Separable Bearings

#### Non-Separable Bearings
- Deep groove ball bearings
- Angular contact ball bearings (most types)
- Self-aligning ball bearings
- Cannot be disassembled without damage

#### Separable Bearings
- Cylindrical roller bearings
- Tapered roller bearings
- Spherical roller bearings
- Thrust bearings
- Inner ring, outer ring, and cage assembly can be mounted separately

## Component Details

### 1. Bearing Rings

#### Inner Ring (Внутреннее кольцо)
**Function:**
- Provides inner raceway for rolling elements
- Typically mounted on shaft with interference fit
- Transmits load from shaft to rolling elements

**Features:**
- Bore diameter (d) - typically matches shaft diameter
- Raceway - precisely ground running surface
- Shoulders - guide and retain rolling elements
- May include mounting grooves or holes

#### Outer Ring (Наружное кольцо)
**Function:**
- Provides outer raceway for rolling elements
- Typically mounted in housing bore
- Transmits load from rolling elements to housing

**Features:**
- Outside diameter (D) - matches housing bore
- Raceway - precisely ground running surface
- Shoulders - guide and retain rolling elements
- May include locating features

### 2. Rolling Elements

#### Balls (Шарики)
**Characteristics:**
- Spherical shape
- Point contact with raceways
- Low friction
- Suitable for high speeds
- Lower load capacity than rollers

**Materials:**
- Through-hardened steel (ГОСТ 801-78, ШХ15)
- Stainless steel (AISI 440C)
- Ceramic (Si₃N₄) for special applications

#### Cylindrical Rollers (Цилиндрические ролики)
**Characteristics:**
- Straight cylindrical shape
- Line contact with raceways
- Higher load capacity than balls
- Lower friction than other roller types
- Primarily radial load capacity

#### Tapered Rollers (Конические ролики)
**Characteristics:**
- Conical shape
- Line contact on tapered raceways
- Can support combined radial and axial loads
- Must be used in pairs or groups
- Adjustable for preload

#### Spherical Rollers (Сферические ролики)
**Characteristics:**
- Barrel-shaped profile
- Self-aligning capability
- High load capacity
- Good for misalignment conditions
- Available in symmetric and asymmetric designs

#### Needle Rollers (Игольчатые ролики)
**Characteristics:**
- Small diameter relative to length
- Very compact radial section
- High load capacity in small space
- Require precise alignment
- Lower speed capability

### 3. Cages (Separators)

#### Functions
- Separate rolling elements to prevent contact and wear
- Guide rolling elements through loaded and unloaded zones
- Reduce friction and heat generation
- Distribute lubricant

#### Cage Types

**By Material:**
- **Steel cages** - stamped or machined, high strength
- **Brass cages** - machined, good heat dissipation
- **Polymer cages** - molded, lightweight, low noise
- **Bronze cages** - machined, high strength and temperature resistance

**By Design:**
- **Stamped cages** - economical, lightweight
- **Machined cages** - precise, robust
- **Molded cages** - complex geometries possible
- **Ribbon cages** - simple, for cylindrical rollers
- **Snap cages** - easy assembly

### 4. Seals and Shields

#### Seals (Уплотнения)
**Contact Seals:**
- Rubber or synthetic material
- Contact with ring surface
- Effective contamination protection
- Retain lubrication
- Slight friction increase
- Lower speed limit

**Non-Contact Seals:**
- Small clearance gap
- Minimal friction
- Good for higher speeds
- Less effective protection

#### Shields (Защитные шайбы)
- Metal construction
- Non-contact design
- Basic protection against large particles
- No friction penalty
- Suitable for high speeds
- Does not retain grease as effectively

### 5. Lubrication

#### Grease Lubrication
**Advantages:**
- Simple sealing
- Long relubrication intervals
- Built-in seals retain grease
- Protection against moisture and contaminants

**Common Greases:**
- Lithium-based (general purpose)
- Polyurea (high temperature, long life)
- NLGI Grade 2 or 3 typical

#### Oil Lubrication
**Advantages:**
- Better heat dissipation
- Higher speed capability
- Longer life in some applications
- Can be filtered and cooled

**Methods:**
- Oil bath
- Circulating oil
- Oil mist
- Oil-air lubrication

## Material and Heat Treatment

### Ring and Rolling Element Materials

**Through-Hardened Bearing Steel:**
- ГОСТ: ШХ15, ШХ15СГ
- International: 100Cr6, SUJ2, 52100
- Hardness: 58-64 HRC
- Standard for most bearings

**Case-Hardened Steel:**
- For large bearings or special applications
- Core toughness with hard surface
- Examples: 18ХГТ, 20Х2Н4А

**Stainless Steel:**
- Corrosion resistance
- Slightly lower load capacity
- AISI 440C typical

**Ceramic:**
- Silicon nitride (Si₃N₄)
- High temperature capability
- Lower density
- Electrical insulation
- Higher cost

### Heat Treatment
- Quenching and tempering
- Target hardness: 58-64 HRC for rings and rolling elements
- Residual austenite control
- Dimensional stability

## Manufacturing Processes

### Rings
1. **Tube or bar stock** - starting material
2. **Turning** - rough machining to near-net shape
3. **Heat treatment** - hardening
4. **Grinding** - precision finishing of raceways, bore, and OD
5. **Superfinishing** - raceway surface finishing (optional)
6. **Honing** - bore finishing (when required)

### Rolling Elements
1. **Wire or bar stock** - high-quality bearing steel
2. **Heading or machining** - forming near-net shape
3. **Heat treatment** - hardening
4. **Grinding and lapping** - precision spherical or cylindrical form
5. **Sorting** - dimensional grading

### Assembly
1. **Component inspection** - dimensions and surface quality
2. **Cage and rolling element assembly**
3. **Ring assembly** - for non-separable types
4. **Seal/shield installation** - if applicable
5. **Lubrication** - grease filling
6. **Final inspection** - noise, vibration, runout

## Quality Control

### Dimensional Inspection
- Bore and outside diameter
- Width
- Raceway geometry
- Rolling element diameter and grade

### Performance Testing
- Noise and vibration
- Running torque
- Radial and axial play
- Grease distribution

### Surface Quality
- Raceway surface finish
- Surface integrity (no cracks, inclusions)
- Hardness verification

# Bearing Defect Terminology

## Purpose
This document provides standardized terminology for bearing defects, surface damage classification, measurement criteria, and acceptance/rejection standards according to ГОСТ, ISO, and manufacturer specifications.

## Overview

Precise defect terminology is essential for:
- Accurate communication between inspection, maintenance, and engineering
- Consistent quality control and acceptance decisions
- Proper documentation for warranty claims
- Root cause analysis
- Trend analysis for reliability improvement

## 1. Surface Damage Terminology

### 1.1 Spalling (Выкрашивание)

**Definition (ISO 15243):**
Detachment of particles from the surface of rolling contact, leaving a crater or pit. Typically results from subsurface fatigue crack propagation to the surface.

**Detailed Characteristics:**
- **Size:** Typically > 1mm in diameter or length
- **Depth:** Extends into subsurface (0.01-1mm depth)
- **Shape:** Irregular, often elongated in rolling direction
- **Edges:** Sharp, well-defined boundaries
- **Bottom:** May show fatigue crack origin point
- **Appearance:** Shiny facets at fracture surfaces

**Terminology Variations:**
- **Russian (ГОСТ):** Выкрашивание
- **German (DIN):** Ausbruch, Abblätterung
- **English variants:** Spalding, flaking (less precise)

**Size Classification:**
- **Incipient spalling:** < 5mm²
- **Small spalling:** 5-50mm²
- **Medium spalling:** 50-200mm²
- **Large spalling:** > 200mm²

**Location Terms:**
- **Raceway spalling:** On inner or outer ring raceway
- **Shoulder spalling:** On ring rib/shoulder
- **Rolling element spalling:** On ball or roller surface
- **Distributed spalling:** Multiple spalls across raceway
- **Localized spalling:** Single or grouped spalls in one area

### 1.2 Pitting (Питтинг)

**Definition (ISO 15243):**
Small craters on the surface, typically smaller and shallower than spalling.

**Detailed Characteristics:**
- **Size:** 0.1-1mm diameter
- **Depth:** Shallow, typically < 0.1mm
- **Distribution:** Often multiple pits clustered or distributed
- **Edges:** Less defined than spalling
- **Appearance:** Matte, frosted surface finish

**Types:**

#### Initial Pitting (Начальный питтинг)
- Occurs during run-in period
- Small pits, may stabilize
- Not necessarily progressive
- Size: 0.1-0.5mm

#### Progressive Pitting (Прогрессирующий питтинг)
- Continues to develop
- Will lead to spalling
- Indicates inadequate lubrication
- Size increases over time

#### Micropitting (Микропиттинг)
- Very fine pits < 10μm
- Creates grey, matte appearance
- Difficult to see without magnification
- Common under thin film lubrication

**ГОСТ 20407-75 Classification:**
- Питтинг поверхностный (surface pitting)
- Питтинг глубокий (deep pitting)

### 1.3 Peeling (Шелушение)

**Definition:**
Thin layer separation from the surface, typically manufacturing-related.

**Detailed Characteristics:**
- **Thickness:** Very thin (< 0.05mm)
- **Appearance:** Flake-like, layered
- **Cause:** Usually heat treatment issues, decarburization
- **Location:** Ground surfaces

**Distinction from Spalling:**
- Shallower
- More uniform thickness
- Typically manufacturing defect rather than service damage
- Often discovered during incoming inspection

**Related Terms:**
- **Delamination:** Layer separation within material
- **Scaling:** Similar appearance, often corrosion-related

### 1.4 Flaking (Отслаивание)

**Definition:**
Detachment of thin surface layers, intermediate between peeling and spalling.

**Characteristics:**
- **Thickness:** 0.05-0.2mm
- **Shape:** Irregular plates or flakes
- **Cause:** Various (fatigue, manufacturing, corrosion)

**Context-Specific Usage:**
- In some literature, used interchangeably with spalling
- FAG distinguishes: flaking = shallow, spalling = deep
- ISO 15243 includes under "surface distress"

### 1.5 Scoring (Задиры)

**Definition:**
Severe surface damage from sliding contact under load with inadequate lubrication.

**Detailed Characteristics:**
- **Appearance:** Deep scratches, grooves
- **Direction:** Aligned with sliding direction
- **Depth:** Significant (0.1-1mm or more)
- **Width:** Varies, typically several mm
- **Associated:** Material transfer, discoloration, roughness

**Severity Levels:**
- **Light scoring:** Visible marks, minimal depth
- **Moderate scoring:** Measurable grooves, surface roughness
- **Severe scoring:** Deep damage, material displacement, heat effects

### 1.6 Scuffing (Задиры, схватывание)

**Definition:**
Adhesive wear damage from metal-to-metal contact and localized welding.

**Characteristics:**
- Material transfer between surfaces
- Rough, torn appearance
- Often accompanies scoring
- Indicates lubrication failure

**Related Terms:**
- **Galling:** Severe scuffing with significant damage
- **Seizing:** Extreme adhesion preventing motion

### 1.7 Scratching (Царапины)

**Definition:**
Narrow surface grooves from hard particles or edges.

**Characteristics:**
- **Width:** Typically < 0.5mm
- **Depth:** Shallow to moderate
- **Pattern:** Random or parallel lines
- **Cause:** Abrasive particles, handling damage

**Classification:**
- **Fine scratches:** < 0.1mm wide, superficial
- **Medium scratches:** 0.1-0.5mm wide, visible
- **Coarse scratches:** > 0.5mm wide, may affect function

### 1.8 Indentation (Вмятины)

**Definition:**
Localized permanent deformation creating a depression.

**Types:**

#### Brinell Marks (Бринеллирование)
- Caused by static overload
- Regular spacing matching rolling elements
- Well-defined edges
- Both raceways and rolling elements affected

#### Debris Indentation (Вмятины от частиц)
- Caused by hard particles pressed into surface
- Irregular spacing and shape
- May show particle embedded
- Random distribution

#### Impact Damage (Ударные вмятины)
- From handling impacts
- Irregular shape and location
- May show material displacement

**Measurement:**
- Depth measurement with profilometer
- Comparison to specified limits
- Documentation of size and location

### 1.9 Discoloration (Изменение цвета)

**Definition:**
Color change of bearing surfaces indicating thermal, chemical, or corrosion damage.

**Color Indicators:**

| Color | Temperature (Approx.) | Implication |
|-------|----------------------|-------------|
| Light straw | 200°C | Normal operating range for some applications |
| Dark straw/yellow | 250°C | Elevated temperature, monitor |
| Brown | 300°C | Overheating, investigate cause |
| Purple/blue | 350°C+ | Severe overheating, likely material damage |
| Black | 400°C+ | Extreme overheating, material degradation |

**Non-Thermal Discoloration:**
- **Red-brown:** Rust, moisture corrosion
- **Black (oxide):** Fretting, false brinelling
- **Grey (dull):** Micropitting, surface fatigue
- **Greenish:** Copper corrosion (brass cages)

**ГОСТ Terminology:**
- Побежалость (temper colors from heating)
- Коррозия (corrosion products)

### 1.10 Wear (Износ)

**Definition:**
Progressive material removal from surfaces in relative motion.

**Types by Mechanism:**
- **Abrasive wear (Абразивный износ):** Hard particles
- **Adhesive wear (Адгезионный износ):** Surface welding
- **Corrosive wear (Коррозионный износ):** Chemical + mechanical
- **Fretting wear (Фреттинг-износ):** Oscillatory micro-motion

**Measurement:**
- Dimensional comparison to original
- Surface roughness increase
- Clearance increase
- Material loss quantification

## 2. Defect Classification by Size and Severity

### 2.1 ISO Severity Classification

**Minor Defects:**
- Do not significantly affect bearing function
- May be acceptable for non-critical applications
- Examples: Fine scratches, light discoloration, minor wear

**Major Defects:**
- Affect bearing performance or life
- May be acceptable with reduced rating or life expectancy
- Examples: Moderate pitting, visible wear, indentations

**Critical Defects:**
- Make bearing unsuitable for use
- Immediate failure risk or rapid deterioration
- Examples: Cracks, large spalls, severe corrosion, cage damage

### 2.2 ГОСТ Classification System

According to ГОСТ 20407-75, defects are classified by:

**Type (Вид дефекта):**
1. Усталостные (Fatigue)
2. Износные (Wear)
3. Коррозионные (Corrosion)
4. Деформационные (Deformation)
5. Производственные (Manufacturing)

**Severity (Степень дефекта):**
- **Степень 1:** Незначительный (insignificant)
- **Степень 2:** Допустимый (permissible)
- **Степень 3:** Предельный (limiting)
- **Степень 4:** Недопустимый (unacceptable)

**Location (Место расположения):**
- Внутренняя дорожка (inner raceway)
- Наружная дорожка (outer raceway)
- Тела качения (rolling elements)
- Сепаратор (cage)
- Торцы колец (ring faces)
- Посадочные поверхности (fitting surfaces)

### 2.3 Dimensional Tolerances for Defects

**Surface Finish Limits:**

| Surface | Normal Ra (μm) | Maximum Acceptable Ra (μm) |
|---------|----------------|----------------------------|
| Raceway (precision) | 0.05-0.1 | 0.2 |
| Raceway (standard) | 0.1-0.2 | 0.4 |
| Rolling element | 0.05-0.08 | 0.15 |
| Seal running surface | 0.2-0.4 | 0.8 |
| Bore/OD (precision) | 0.4-0.8 | 1.6 |

**Spall Size Limits (General Guideline):**
- **Acceptable:** None (for new bearings)
- **Monitoring required:** < 1mm² (service inspection)
- **Action required:** 1-10mm²
- **Immediate replacement:** > 10mm²

**Note:** Actual limits depend on application criticality, bearing size, and manufacturer specifications.

### 2.4 Cage Defect Criteria

**Wear Limits:**
- Pocket wear: < 10% of original material thickness
- Rivet looseness: Not acceptable
- Cracks: Not acceptable
- Material loss: < 5% of cross-section

**Deformation Limits:**
- Out-of-roundness: < 0.5mm for diameter > 100mm
- Twist: Not acceptable
- Pocket elongation: < 0.2mm

## 3. Defect Location Terminology

### 3.1 Raceway Defects

**Circumferential Position:**
- **Load zone (зона нагрузки):** Primary load-carrying region
- **Unloaded zone (ненагруженная зона):** Region opposite to load
- **Entry zone:** Rolling elements entering load zone
- **Exit zone:** Rolling elements leaving load zone

**Axial Position:**
- **Center of raceway (центр дорожки):** Mid-width of raceway
- **Edge of raceway (край дорожки):** Near shoulder
- **Full width (по всей ширине):** Across entire raceway width

**Angular Description:**
- Use clock position (e.g., 3 o'clock position)
- Or degrees from reference mark (0-360°)
- Specify if stationary or rotating ring

### 3.2 Ring Defects

**Inner Ring Locations:**
- **Bore (посадочное отверстие):** Mounting surface
- **Raceway (дорожка качения):** Rolling surface
- **Shoulder/rib (бортик):** Axial guide surface
- **Face (торец):** Axial end surface
- **Groove (канавка):** Locknut groove, snap ring groove

**Outer Ring Locations:**
- **Outside diameter (наружная поверхность):** Housing fit surface
- **Raceway (дорожка качения):** Rolling surface
- **Shoulder/rib (бортик):** Axial guide surface
- **Face (торец):** Axial end surface
- **Locating features (элементы фиксации):** Grooves, holes, slots

### 3.3 Rolling Element Defects

**Ball Defects:**
- **Equator (экваториальная зона):** Main contact band
- **Pole (полюс):** Top and bottom regions
- **Surface (поверхность):** General surface area

**Roller Defects:**
- **Central section (центральная часть):** Main load-bearing area
- **End (торец):** Roller end face
- **Flange contact (контакт с бортиком):** Contact area with rib
- **Circumference (по окружности):** Around roller

### 3.4 Cage Defects

**Location Terms:**
- **Pocket (гнездо, ячейка):** Element retention area
- **Bar/pillar (перемычка):** Structural member between pockets
- **Ring/rail (обод):** Circumferential structure
- **Rivet (заклепка):** Fastener (if riveted construction)
- **Guiding surface (направляющая поверхность):** Ring or roller guided surface

## 4. ГОСТ Defect Classification System

### 4.1 ГОСТ 20407-75 Defect Categories

**1. Усталостные дефекты (Fatigue Defects):**

1.1. **Выкрашивание (Spalling)**
- Выкрашивание дорожки качения (raceway spalling)
- Выкрашивание тел качения (rolling element spalling)
- Начальное выкрашивание (initial spalling)
- Прогрессирующее выкрашивание (progressive spalling)

1.2. **Питтинг (Pitting)**
- Поверхностный питтинг (surface pitting)
- Глубокий питтинг (deep pitting)

1.3. **Трещины усталостные (Fatigue cracks)**
- Кольцевые трещины (circumferential cracks)
- Радиальные трещины (radial cracks)
- Сетка трещин (crack network)

**2. Износные дефекты (Wear Defects):**

2.1. **Абразивный износ (Abrasive wear)**
- Износ дорожек качения (raceway wear)
- Износ тел качения (rolling element wear)
- Износ сепаратора (cage wear)

2.2. **Адгезионный износ (Adhesive wear)**
- Смазывание (smearing)
- Задиры (scoring)
- Схватывание (scuffing)

2.3. **Коррозионный износ (Corrosive wear)**
- Combined mechanical and chemical action

**3. Коррозионные дефекты (Corrosion Defects):**

3.1. **Общая коррозия (General corrosion)**
- Ржавчина (rust)
- Окисление (oxidation)

3.2. **Фреттинг-коррозия (Fretting corrosion)**
- На посадочных поверхностях (on fits)
- На торцах (on faces)

3.3. **Электроэрозия (Electrical erosion)**
- Флютинг (fluting)
- Точечная эрозия (pitting erosion)

**4. Деформационные дефекты (Deformation Defects):**

4.1. **Бринеллирование (Brinelling)**
- Истинное бринеллирование (true brinelling)
- Ложное бринеллирование (false brinelling)

4.2. **Вмятины (Indentations)**
- Вмятины от частиц (debris indentation)
- Ударные вмятины (impact damage)

4.3. **Пластическая деформация (Plastic deformation)**
- Смятие (crushing)
- Деформация колец (ring deformation)

**5. Производственные дефекты (Manufacturing Defects):**

5.1. **Шлифовочные дефекты (Grinding defects)**
- Прижоги (grinding burns)
- Шлифовочные трещины (grinding cracks)
- Волнистость (waviness)

5.2. **Термообработка (Heat treatment defects)**
- Неправильная твердость (incorrect hardness)
- Обезуглероживание (decarburization)
- Мягкие пятна (soft spots)

5.3. **Материальные дефекты (Material defects)**
- Включения (inclusions)
- Пористость (porosity)
- Структурные дефекты (structural defects)

### 4.2 ГОСТ Documentation Requirements

**Defect Record Must Include:**
1. Тип дефекта (defect type) - from classification
2. Место расположения (location) - ring, position
3. Размер дефекта (size) - dimensions
4. Степень развития (severity) - stage of development
5. Количество (quantity) - number of defects
6. Сопутствующие дефекты (associated defects)

**Sketch/Photo Requirements:**
- Scale indication
- Reference orientation
- Defect clearly marked
- Multiple views if necessary

## 5. ISO Defect Classification System

### 5.1 ISO 15243:2017 Structure

**Main Categories:**

**1. Fatigue**
- Surface-originated fatigue
- Subsurface-originated fatigue
- Rolling contact fatigue (typical progression)
- Irregular fatigue patterns

**2. Wear**
- Abrasive wear
- Adhesive wear
- Smearing
- False brinelling (wear variant)

**3. Corrosion**
- Moisture corrosion
- Fretting corrosion
- Etching
- Lubricant-related corrosion

**4. Electrical Erosion**
- Fluting (corrugation pattern)
- Frosting (pitting pattern)

**5. Plastic Deformation**
- Indentations from overload (true brinelling)
- Indentations from debris
- Overrolling damage

**6. Fracture and Cracking**
- Forced fracture (overload)
- Fatigue fracture
- Thermal cracking

**Comparison to ГОСТ:**
- Similar structure but different organization
- ISO more detailed in some areas (electrical erosion)
- ГОСТ separates manufacturing defects more clearly
- Both systems compatible with cross-referencing

### 5.2 ISO Defect Codes (Manufacturer Systems)

**SKF Damage Codes (example):**
- Code structure: Letter + Number
- F1, F2, F3... = Fatigue variants
- W1, W2, W3... = Wear variants
- C1, C2, C3... = Corrosion variants
- D1, D2, D3... = Deformation variants

**FAG System:**
- Similar coding approach
- Cross-referenced to ISO 15243

**Note:** Manufacturer-specific codes vary; always reference their documentation.

## 6. Surface Finish Defects

### 6.1 Roughness (Шероховатость)

**Parameters:**
- **Ra (average roughness):** Arithmetic mean deviation
- **Rz (ten-point height):** Average peak-to-valley height
- **Rmax:** Maximum peak-to-valley height

**Bearing Surface Specifications:**

| Surface Type | Typical Ra (μm) | Precision Ra (μm) |
|--------------|-----------------|-------------------|
| Raceway (ball bearing) | 0.05-0.10 | 0.02-0.05 |
| Raceway (roller bearing) | 0.10-0.20 | 0.05-0.10 |
| Ball surface | 0.05-0.08 | 0.02-0.05 |
| Roller surface | 0.08-0.15 | 0.05-0.10 |
| Bore/OD (mounting) | 0.4-0.8 | 0.2-0.4 |
| Ring faces | 1.0-2.5 | 0.4-1.0 |

**ГОСТ Designation:**
- Ra values per ГОСТ 2789-73
- Surface finish symbols per ГОСТ 2.309-73

### 6.2 Waviness (Волнистость)

**Definition:**
Surface irregularities with wavelength longer than roughness but shorter than geometric errors.

**Characteristics:**
- Wavelength: Typically 0.5-50mm
- Amplitude: Measured in μm
- Orientation: Circumferential or axial

**Causes:**
- Grinding wheel imbalance
- Machine tool vibration
- Workpiece vibration
- Dressing issues

**Effects:**
- Vibration at specific frequencies
- Noise generation
- Reduced fatigue life
- Non-uniform load distribution

**Measurement:**
- Specialized waviness measurement instruments
- Filtered profilometer traces
- Specified in bearing standards for precision classes

### 6.3 Grinding Marks (Следы шлифования)

**Types:**

**Circumferential Marks (Feed marks):**
- From grinding wheel traverse
- Spiral pattern on raceway
- Normal manufacturing characteristic
- Should be within roughness specification

**Radial Marks (Chatter marks):**
- From vibration during grinding
- Parallel grooves
- May indicate manufacturing problem
- Can cause premature fatigue

**Crosshatch Marks:**
- From improper grinding technique
- Random or intersecting patterns
- Not acceptable for precision bearings

### 6.4 Grinding Burns (Прижоги)

**Definition:**
Localized overheating during grinding causing material transformation.

**Visual Characteristics:**
- Discoloration (blue, brown, black spots)
- Typically on raceways
- May be subtle or obvious

**Material Effects:**
- Untempered martensite formation
- Reduced hardness locally
- Residual tensile stress
- Micro-cracking (grinding cracks)

**Detection:**
- Visual inspection (discoloration)
- Acid etching reveals microstructure changes
- Hardness testing
- Magnetic particle inspection

**Acceptance:**
- Generally not acceptable for new bearings
- Indicates manufacturing quality issue
- High risk of premature failure

## 7. Material Defects

### 7.1 Inclusions (Включения)

**Definition:**
Foreign particles within the steel matrix.

**Types:**
- **Oxides:** Most common, various compositions
- **Sulfides:** Manganese sulfide (MnS)
- **Silicates:** Complex oxide inclusions
- **Carbides:** Typically chromium carbides (in bearing steel)

**Characteristics:**
- **Size:** μm to mm range
- **Shape:** Spherical, elongated, clustered
- **Cleanliness rating:** Per ASTM E45 or similar

**Effects:**
- Stress concentration sites
- Fatigue crack initiation
- Reduced bearing life
- More critical near surface

**Inspection:**
- Metallographic examination
- Ultrasonic testing (large inclusions)
- X-ray inspection
- Statistical sampling

**Standards:**
- ISO 683-17 (Steel cleanliness)
- ASTM E45 (Inclusion rating)
- DIN 50602 (Microscopic examination)

### 7.2 Heat Treatment Defects

**Decarburization (Обезуглероживание):**
- Surface carbon depletion
- Reduced surface hardness
- Depth: Typically < 0.1mm
- Detection: Hardness traverse, microstructure examination
- Limit: Usually < 0.02mm for bearing quality

**Soft Spots (Мягкие пятна):**
- Localized areas of insufficient hardness
- Caused by inadequate quenching
- Detection: Hardness mapping, visual (often lighter)
- Not acceptable for critical surfaces

**Excessive Carbide Network:**
- Undissolved carbides in grain boundaries
- Reduces toughness
- Can cause brittle fracture
- Controlled by spheroidizing and austenitizing

**Retained Austenite:**
- Austenite not transformed to martensite
- Reduces hardness and dimensional stability
- Typical acceptable range: 3-8%
- Measured by X-ray diffraction or magnetic saturation

**Hardness Specifications:**
- Raceways: 58-65 HRC (typical)
- Core: 25-45 HRC (depending on design)
- Gradient: Gradual transition

### 7.3 Microstructure Defects

**Untempered Martensite:**
- From grinding burns or improper heat treatment
- Very brittle, prone to cracking
- Detected by metallography and hardness

**Coarse Grain Structure:**
- Excessive austenite grain size
- Reduces toughness
- Measured per ASTM E112

**Non-Uniform Structure:**
- Inconsistent heat treatment
- Variable properties
- Quality control failure

## 8. Visual Inspection Criteria

### 8.1 Inspection Conditions

**Lighting:**
- Minimum: 500 lux
- Recommended: 1000 lux for detailed inspection
- Type: Diffuse lighting to avoid glare
- Angle: Adjustable for surface examination

**Magnification:**
- **Naked eye:** General inspection, large defects
- **2-5x:** Detailed surface examination
- **10-20x:** Micro-defect inspection
- **50x+:** Metallurgical examination

**Cleanliness:**
- Bearing must be thoroughly cleaned
- Solvent wash followed by air dry
- No lubricant or preservative on inspection surfaces
- White gloves recommended to avoid contamination

### 8.2 Inspection Procedure

**Step 1: Preliminary Visual Inspection**
- Check overall condition
- Look for obvious damage (cracks, large spalls, corrosion)
- Check cage integrity
- Verify seal condition

**Step 2: Detailed Surface Inspection**
- Examine each raceway systematically
- Rotate bearing to view full circumference
- Check rolling elements individually
- Inspect ring faces and mounting surfaces

**Step 3: Dimensional Checks**
- Measure clearance (radial and axial if applicable)
- Check bore and OD dimensions
- Verify width/height
- Compare to specifications

**Step 4: Functional Check**
- Rotate bearing by hand
- Feel for roughness or resistance
- Listen for noise
- Check for excessive play

**Step 5: Documentation**
- Record all defects found
- Photograph or sketch significant damage
- Complete inspection form
- Assign disposition (accept, monitor, reject)

### 8.3 Acceptance Criteria (General Guidelines)

**New Bearings (Quality Control):**
- **Raceways:** No spalling, pitting, cracks, or corrosion
- **Surface finish:** Within specification (Ra limits)
- **Geometry:** Within tolerance class
- **Cleanliness:** No contamination, proper preservation
- **Cage:** No damage, proper assembly
- **Seals/shields:** Properly installed, no damage

**Used Bearings (Service Inspection):**

| Condition | Action |
|-----------|--------|
| No defects, normal wear | Continue operation |
| Minor pitting, light discoloration | Monitor, increase inspection frequency |
| Initial spalling < 5mm² | Plan replacement at next maintenance |
| Spalling > 5mm², cracks, heavy pitting | Replace immediately |
| Cage damage, corrosion | Replace immediately |

**Application-Specific Variations:**
- Critical applications (aerospace, medical): Stricter criteria
- Non-critical applications: More tolerance for minor defects
- Consult manufacturer recommendations
- Consider operating conditions and remaining life

## 9. Measurement and Documentation

### 9.1 Measurement Techniques

**Surface Roughness:**
- **Instrument:** Contact profilometer (stylus type)
- **Standards:** ISO 4287, ISO 4288
- **Parameters:** Ra, Rz, Rmax
- **Locations:** Multiple points on raceway, average values

**Dimensional Measurements:**
- **Bore/OD:** Micrometer, ID/OD gauge
- **Width:** Caliper, micrometer
- **Clearance:** Dial indicator method or pneumatic gauge
- **Roundness:** Roundness measuring machine

**Defect Size:**
- **Length/width:** Caliper, scale with magnifier
- **Depth:** Depth micrometer, profilometer
- **Area:** Estimation or image analysis software

**Hardness:**
- **Method:** Rockwell C scale (HRC)
- **Locations:** Raceway surface, case depth profile
- **Standards:** ISO 6508, ASTM E18

### 9.2 Documentation Standards

**Inspection Report Should Include:**

1. **Bearing Identification:**
   - Designation (e.g., 6205-2RS)
   - Serial number (if available)
   - Manufacturer
   - Date of manufacture (if known)

2. **Application Information:**
   - Machine/equipment type
   - Operating hours
   - Operating conditions (load, speed, temperature)
   - Lubrication type and schedule

3. **Defect Documentation:**
   - Type of defect (using standard terminology)
   - Location (ring, position)
   - Size (dimensions)
   - Severity assessment
   - Photographs or sketches
   - Quantity (if multiple defects)

4. **Measurements:**
   - Critical dimensions
   - Clearance values
   - Surface roughness (if measured)
   - Comparison to original specifications

5. **Assessment:**
   - Root cause analysis (preliminary)
   - Severity classification
   - Recommendation (continue, monitor, replace)
   - Estimated remaining life (if applicable)

6. **Inspector Information:**
   - Name and qualification
   - Date of inspection
   - Signature/approval

### 9.3 Photography Guidelines

**Equipment:**
- Minimum 12 megapixel camera
- Macro lens capability
- Ring light or diffuse lighting
- Tripod for stability

**Technique:**
- **Scale reference:** Include ruler or scale in frame
- **Orientation:** Mark and document orientation
- **Focus:** Ensure defect is sharp and clear
- **Lighting:** Avoid glare and shadows
- **Multiple views:** Overall and close-up
- **Consistency:** Same setup for comparison over time

**Image Management:**
- File naming convention (bearing ID, location, date)
- Organize by bearing or by date
- Backup and archive
- Include metadata (camera settings, magnification)

## 10. Rejection Criteria

### 10.1 New Bearing Inspection (Incoming Quality)

**Mandatory Rejection Criteria:**
1. **Cracks:** Any crack on raceways, rolling elements, or structural areas
2. **Spalling:** Any spalling on functional surfaces
3. **Corrosion:** Visible rust or corrosion on raceways
4. **Dimensional errors:** Outside tolerance class specification
5. **Grinding burns:** Visible discoloration on raceways
6. **Hardness deviations:** Outside specified range (58-65 HRC typical)
7. **Cage damage:** Cracks, severe deformation, missing parts
8. **Contamination:** Embedded particles, heavy soiling
9. **Seal damage:** Torn, displaced, or improperly installed seals

**Conditional Rejection (Consult Manufacturer):**
1. Minor surface marks not on raceways
2. Light handling marks on non-functional surfaces
3. Storage marks (if properly preserved and within storage life)

### 10.2 Used Bearing Inspection (Service)

**Immediate Replacement Required:**
1. **Any cracks** - risk of catastrophic failure
2. **Spalling > 10mm²** - rapid deterioration imminent
3. **Severe pitting** - covering > 10% of raceway
4. **Cage damage** - broken, cracked, severely worn
5. **Corrosion** - deep pitting, significant material loss
6. **Excessive clearance** - beyond maximum specified
7. **Overheating evidence** - blue/black discoloration on raceways
8. **Seal failure** - with contamination ingress evident

**Planned Replacement (Next Scheduled Maintenance):**
1. **Initial spalling** - small, localized (< 10mm²)
2. **Moderate pitting** - distributed but not severe
3. **Wear** - measurable but within operational limits
4. **Light corrosion** - surface staining, no deep pitting
5. **Cage wear** - visible but not severe

**Continue with Monitoring:**
1. **Minor surface marks** - not progressive
2. **Light discoloration** - no indication of current overheating
3. **Normal wear** - within expected range for operating time
4. **Adequate clearance** - within operational range

### 10.3 Application-Specific Criteria

**Critical Applications (Safety, High Consequence of Failure):**
- Very low tolerance for defects
- Preventive replacement at first sign of degradation
- Examples: Aircraft, medical equipment, elevators

**High-Reliability Applications (Difficult to Service):**
- Conservative replacement criteria
- Plan replacement before critical defects develop
- Examples: Remote installations, continuous process equipment

**Standard Industrial Applications:**
- Balance reliability with economics
- Replace when defects indicate approaching end of life
- Monitor and trend condition

**Non-Critical Applications:**
- Run to failure may be acceptable
- Cost-benefit analysis for replacement
- Examples: Non-essential equipment, redundant systems

# Everest B2B Content Skill

## When to use

Use this skill for:

- website copy;
- product cards;
- commercial descriptions;
- catalog text;
- brand book text;
- Telegram/VK/website posts;
- B2B sales materials;
- descriptions of ООО «Эверест» as a supplier of bearings and industrial components.

## Positioning

ООО «Эверест» is positioned as a B2B supplier of bearings and industrial components for enterprises. The company helps with standard, urgent, non-standard and complex requests, GOST/ISO/import analog selection, work by specifications and supply with required accompanying documents.

## Voice

Use:

- precise industrial B2B language;
- calm commercial confidence;
- engineering clarity;
- short useful blocks;
- no hype;
- no unsupported promises.

## Do not use

Avoid:

- `лучшие цены`;
- `гарантированное наличие` unless confirmed;
- `самые быстрые сроки`;
- generic corporate clichés;
- playful consumer tone;
- cheap marketing exaggeration.

## Visual direction

For visual briefs use:

- engineering precision;
- premium B2B;
- Swiss grid;
- clean structured layout;
- German catalog aesthetic;
- industrial editorial;
- white / light gray backgrounds;
- black or graphite typography;
- signal orange accent;
- realistic bearing/product visuals.

Avoid:

- cheap gradients;
- random decoration;
- overloaded AI look;
- template corporate visuals;
- unverified product claims.

## Workflow

1. Define audience: procurement, engineer, production, repair service, management.
2. Define goal: trust, request, specification, selection, document, conversion.
3. Use facts only.
4. Keep claim level controlled.
5. Add next action.
6. Mark unknown data instead of inventing.

## Output pattern

```text
Суть
Что предлагаем / что просим
Условия / ограничения
Следующее действие
```

## Validation checklist

- no unsupported claims;
- B2B tone preserved;
- text is ready to paste;
- no consumer-style advertising;
- no invented stock, price, country, certificates or deadlines.

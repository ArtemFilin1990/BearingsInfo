# Bearing Classification

## Purpose
This document provides a comprehensive classification system for rolling bearings by load direction, design type, and application.

## Classification by Load Direction

### 1. Radial Bearings (Радиальные подшипники)
**Primary Load Direction:** Perpendicular to bearing axis

**Characteristics:**
- Designed primarily for radial loads
- Some types can accommodate limited axial loads
- Most common bearing category

**Examples:**
- Deep groove ball bearings
- Cylindrical roller bearings
- Needle roller bearings
- Spherical roller bearings

### 2. Thrust Bearings (Упорные подшипники)
**Primary Load Direction:** Parallel to bearing axis

**Characteristics:**
- Designed primarily for axial (thrust) loads
- Limited or no radial load capacity
- Often used in vertical shaft applications

**Examples:**
- Thrust ball bearings
- Cylindrical roller thrust bearings
- Spherical roller thrust bearings

### 3. Combined Load Bearings (Комбинированные подшипники)
**Load Direction:** Both radial and axial simultaneously

**Characteristics:**
- Designed for simultaneous radial and axial loads
- Contact angle enables thrust capacity
- Versatile applications

**Examples:**
- Angular contact ball bearings
- Tapered roller bearings
- Spherical roller bearings

## Classification by Rolling Element Type

### Ball Bearings (Шариковые подшипники)

**Characteristics:**
- Point contact between ball and raceway
- Lower friction
- Higher speed capability
- Lower load capacity than roller bearings
- Available in various designs

**Types:**
- Deep groove ball bearings
- Angular contact ball bearings
- Self-aligning ball bearings
- Thrust ball bearings

### Roller Bearings (Роликовые подшипники)

**Characteristics:**
- Line contact between roller and raceway
- Higher load capacity
- Lower speed capability than ball bearings
- Various roller shapes for different applications

**Types:**
- Cylindrical roller bearings
- Tapered roller bearings
- Spherical roller bearings
- Needle roller bearings

## Main Bearing Types

### 1. Deep Groove Ball Bearings (Шариковые радиальные однорядные)

**GOST Designation:** 0, 60, 61, 62, 63, 64, 160, 180 series

**Characteristics:**
- Most common bearing type
- Deep raceway grooves (~30% of ball diameter)
- Low friction, low noise
- High speed capability
- Can support some axial load in both directions

**Applications:**
- Electric motors
- Gearboxes
- Automotive components
- General machinery

**Variants:**
- Open (without seals)
- Shielded (Z, ZZ)
- Sealed (RS, 2RS)
- With snap ring (N, NR)

### 2. Angular Contact Ball Bearings (Шариковые радиальные-упорные)

**GOST Designation:** 36, 46, 66, 76 series

**Characteristics:**
- Contact angle: 15°, 25°, 40°
- High axial load capacity in one direction
- Used in pairs or sets for bidirectional loads
- Higher speed than tapered roller bearings

**Arrangements:**
- Back-to-back (DB) - high moment capacity
- Face-to-face (DF) - less common
- Tandem (DT) - double axial capacity

**Applications:**
- Machine tool spindles
- Automotive wheel hubs
- Compressors and pumps
- Precision applications

### 3. Self-Aligning Ball Bearings (Шариковые радиальные сферические двухрядные)

**GOST Designation:** 1200, 1300 series

**Characteristics:**
- Spherical outer raceway
- Allows angular misalignment: 1.5° - 3°
- Two rows of balls
- Moderate load capacity
- Good for shaft deflection

**Applications:**
- Agricultural machinery
- Conveyors
- Wood processing equipment
- Applications with shaft misalignment

### 4. Cylindrical Roller Bearings (Роликовые с короткими цилиндрическими роликами)

**GOST Designation:** 2000, 42000, 32000, 3000, 92000 series

**Characteristics:**
- High radial load capacity
- Moderate speed capability
- Separable design
- Low friction
- Some designs allow axial displacement

**Designs:**
- NU - outer ring with ribs, inner ring without ribs
- N - inner ring with ribs, outer ring without ribs
- NJ - outer ring with ribs, inner ring with one rib
- NUP - NJ + loose rib on inner ring

**Applications:**
- Gearboxes
- Electric motors (larger sizes)
- Railway axleboxes
- Rolling mills

### 5. Needle Roller Bearings (Роликовые игольчатые)

**GOST Designation:** 4000, 5000 series

**Characteristics:**
- Very small cross-section
- High radial load capacity
- Low speed capability
- Length/diameter ratio > 2.5
- Require precise alignment

**Types:**
- With inner ring
- Without inner ring (shaft serves as inner raceway)
- Drawn cup
- Caged assemblies

**Applications:**
- Connecting rods
- Rocker arms
- Universal joints
- Compact gearboxes

### 6. Tapered Roller Bearings (Роликовые конические)

**GOST Designation:** 7000, 27000, 97000 series

**Characteristics:**
- Conical rollers and raceways
- High radial and axial load capacity
- Separable (cup and cone)
- Must be adjusted for preload
- Moderate speed

**Arrangements:**
- Single row - one direction thrust
- Double row - bidirectional thrust
- Four-row - heavy loads (rolling mills)

**Applications:**
- Automotive wheel hubs
- Gearboxes
- Heavy machinery
- Railway applications

### 7. Spherical Roller Bearings (Роликовые сферические двухрядные)

**GOST Designation:** 3500, 3600 series

**Characteristics:**
- Self-aligning: 1° - 2.5°
- Very high load capacity
- Two rows of barrel-shaped rollers
- Spherical outer raceway
- Moderate to low speed

**Applications:**
- Crushers and mills
- Paper machines
- Mining equipment
- Heavy industrial machinery

### 8. Thrust Ball Bearings (Шариковые упорные)

**GOST Designation:** 8000, 9000 series

**Characteristics:**
- Pure axial load capacity
- Minimal radial load capability
- Single or double direction
- High speed capability
- Require precise alignment

**Types:**
- Single direction (one washer + housing washer)
- Double direction (two washers + center washer)

**Applications:**
- Crane hooks
- Vertical shaft machinery
- Automotive clutches
- Machine tool spindles (light axial loads)

### 9. Cylindrical Roller Thrust Bearings (Роликовые упорные с цилиндрическими роликами)

**GOST Designation:** Series 81100, 81200

**Characteristics:**
- Very high axial load capacity
- No radial load capacity
- Lower speed than thrust ball bearings
- Separable design

**Applications:**
- Screw presses
- Vertical pumps
- Crane turntables
- Heavy thrust loads

### 10. Spherical Roller Thrust Bearings (Роликовые упорные сферические)

**GOST Designation:** 9000 series (special)

**Characteristics:**
- Very high axial load capacity
- Can support some radial load
- Self-aligning capability
- Asymmetric roller design

**Applications:**
- Hydro turbines
- Marine propeller shafts
- Large vertical motors
- Heavy combined loads

## Classification by Design Features

### Sealed vs Open
- **Open bearings** - require external sealing
- **Shielded (Z, ZZ)** - non-contact metal shields
- **Sealed (RS, 2RS)** - contact rubber seals
- **Lubricated for life** - sealed with grease, no maintenance

### Precision Classes
- **Standard (P0/Class 0)** - general applications
- **P6/Class 6** - improved precision
- **P5/Class 5** - precision applications
- **P4/Class 4** - high precision (machine tools)
- **P2/Class 2** - ultra-precision (special applications)

### Material Variations
- **Standard steel** - through-hardened bearing steel
- **Stainless steel** - corrosion resistance
- **Hybrid (ceramic balls)** - high speed, low friction
- **Full ceramic** - extreme conditions

### Special Features
- **Snap ring groove** - axial location
- **Filling notch** - increased ball complement
- **Locating snap ring** - for outer ring positioning
- **Integral flange** - simplified mounting

## Selection Decision Tree

```
1. What is the primary load direction?
   ├─ Radial only → Deep groove ball, Cylindrical roller, Needle roller
   ├─ Axial only → Thrust ball, Cylindrical roller thrust
   └─ Combined → Angular contact ball, Tapered roller, Spherical roller

2. Is space limited?
   ├─ Radial space limited → Needle roller bearings
   ├─ Axial space limited → Thin section bearings
   └─ No limitations → Standard bearings

3. Is misalignment expected?
   ├─ Yes → Self-aligning ball, Spherical roller
   └─ No → Continue to next question

4. What is the speed requirement?
   ├─ Very high → Deep groove ball, Angular contact ball
   ├─ High → Cylindrical roller
   ├─ Moderate → Tapered roller
   └─ Low → Spherical roller

5. What is the load magnitude?
   ├─ Very high → Spherical roller, Tapered roller
   ├─ High → Cylindrical roller
   ├─ Moderate → Deep groove ball (larger sizes)
   └─ Low → Deep groove ball (smaller sizes)

6. Are there special requirements?
   ├─ Corrosion resistance → Stainless steel bearings
   ├─ High temperature → Special materials/lubricants
   ├─ Low friction → Hybrid bearings
   ├─ No maintenance → Sealed, greased-for-life
   └─ None → Standard bearing with appropriate seals/lubrication
```

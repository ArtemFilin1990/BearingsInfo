# Bearing Storage and Handling

## Purpose
This document provides comprehensive guidance on proper bearing storage, handling, preservation, and preparation for installation to ensure bearings remain in optimal condition until use.

## Storage Environment Requirements

### Temperature Control

**Optimal Storage Temperature:**
- **Recommended: +15°C to +25°C** (59-77°F)
- Acceptable range: +5°C to +30°C (41-86°F)
- Avoid extreme temperatures
- Minimize temperature fluctuations

**Temperature Considerations:**

**Low Temperature (< 5°C):**
- Risk of condensation when moved to warm area
- Grease may harden (pre-lubricated bearings)
- Packaging may become brittle
- **Mitigation:** Allow gradual temperature equalization before opening

**High Temperature (> 30°C):**
- Accelerated lubricant degradation (sealed bearings)
- Preservation compound softening/melting
- Increased corrosion risk
- **Mitigation:** Climate control, cooler storage area

**Temperature Fluctuations:**
- Cause condensation (moisture)
- Rust risk
- **Prevention:** Stable storage environment

### Humidity Control

**Optimal Relative Humidity:**
- **Recommended: 40-60% RH**
- Maximum: 70% RH
- Minimum: 30% RH (very dry can cause static, packaging issues)

**High Humidity Risks:**
- Condensation on metal surfaces
- Rust and corrosion
- Packaging deterioration
- Accelerated degradation

**Humidity Control Methods:**

**Dehumidification:**
- Dehumidifiers in storage room
- Target: 50% RH ± 10%
- Monitor continuously

**Desiccants:**
- Silica gel packets in packaging
- Activated clay desiccants
- Indicator desiccants (color change when saturated)
- Replace when saturated

**Vapor Corrosion Inhibitors (VCI):**
- VCI paper wrapping
- VCI bags or films
- Releases protective vapor
- Excellent for long-term storage

### Storage Location

**Indoor Storage (Required):**
- Clean, dry warehouse or storage room
- Protected from elements
- Temperature and humidity controlled
- Good ventilation (but not drafty)

**Storage Area Characteristics:**

**Avoid:**
- Direct sunlight (UV degradation, temperature variations)
- Proximity to chemicals (acids, solvents, chlorides)
- Near steam lines, heat sources
- Damp areas (basements without dehumidification)
- Areas with vibration
- Dusty or dirty environments
- Near machining or grinding operations

**Good Practices:**
- Elevated storage (off ground)
- Shelving or pallet racks
- Clean floors
- Pest control (prevents nesting in packaging)
- Access control (minimize handling)

### Cleanliness Requirements

**Storage Area Cleanliness:**
- Regular cleaning and housekeeping
- No dust accumulation
- Free from metal chips, grinding dust
- No chemical fumes or corrosive atmospheres

**Air Quality:**
- Filtered air ventilation (if HVAC)
- No salt spray (coastal areas - special consideration)
- No corrosive gases (Cl₂, SO₂, NH₃)
- Low particulate count

**Contamination Sources to Avoid:**
- Concrete dust (from floor cutting, etc.)
- Paint spray overspray
- Welding fumes
- Coolant mist from machining
- Brake dust (automotive shops)

## Preservation and Protection Methods

### Factory Preservation

**Standard Preservation:**

**Light Preservation (C0):**
- Thin film of mineral oil
- Rust protection: 6-12 months (controlled environment)
- Easy to remove (kerosene, white spirit)
- Standard for most bearings

**Medium Preservation (C1):**
- Thicker oil or light grease coating
- Rust protection: 1-2 years
- Requires warm solvent or cleaning agent

**Heavy Preservation (C2):**
- Thick grease or wax-based compound
- Rust protection: 2-3 years
- May require heating to remove
- For long-term storage or export

**Pre-Lubricated/Sealed Bearings:**
- Grease-filled and sealed at factory
- No additional preservation needed
- Do not wash before installation
- Storage life: 5-7 years typical

### Preservation Inspection

**When Receiving Bearings:**

1. **Packaging Integrity:**
   - Check for damage, tears, or punctures
   - Verify moisture indicators (if present)
   - Look for signs of water damage

2. **Preservation Condition:**
   - Preservation coating intact
   - No rust or discoloration
   - Wrapping paper not deteriorated
   - No odor of degradation

3. **Documentation:**
   - Packing list matches received items
   - Bearing designations correct
   - Manufacturing date code (if visible)

**Action if Compromised:**
- Inspect bearing immediately
- Re-preserve if coating damaged but bearing OK
- Return if bearing shows corrosion or damage
- Document condition

### Re-Preservation

**When to Re-Preserve:**
- After inspection if not installing immediately
- Before extended storage (> 6 months)
- If original preservation compromised
- After cleaning for inspection

**Re-Preservation Procedure:**

1. **Cleaning:**
   - Remove old preservation with appropriate solvent
   - Dry thoroughly with clean, dry compressed air
   - Ensure completely free of moisture

2. **Application:**
   - Apply rust-preventive oil or grease
   - Ensure complete coverage (all surfaces)
   - Use spray, brush, or dip method
   - Rotate bearing to distribute preservative

3. **Recommended Preservatives:**
   - Light mineral oil with rust inhibitors
   - Tectyl 506 (spray)
   - Mobilarma 798
   - Shell Ensis Fluid
   - Rustilo DW series
   - VCI oils

4. **Re-Wrapping:**
   - Wrap in VCI paper
   - Seal in moisture-proof bag
   - Add desiccant packet
   - Label with re-preservation date

**Re-Preservation Interval:**
- Inspect every 6-12 months
- Re-preserve if coating deteriorated
- For very long storage (> 3 years), re-preserve annually

## Packaging and Wrapping

### Individual Bearing Packaging

**Small Bearings (bore < 80 mm):**

**Standard Packaging:**
- Individual plastic bags or boxes
- Light preservation coating
- Sometimes inner VCI wrap
- Outer cardboard box

**Long-Term Storage Packaging:**
- VCI poly bag
- Heat-sealed or zip-lock
- Desiccant sachet inside
- Outer protective box

**Medium Bearings (bore 80-200 mm):**

**Standard:**
- Heavy plastic wrap or bag
- Medium preservation
- Sometimes wooden box
- Desiccant included

**Premium/Export:**
- VCI film wrap
- Moisture barrier bag
- Desiccant
- Rigid container (wood, plastic, metal)

**Large Bearings (bore > 200 mm):**

**Standard:**
- Heavy preservation
- Thick plastic or wax paper wrap
- Wooden crate or pallet
- Strapped securely

**For Shipping/Long Storage:**
- VCI wrap + moisture barrier
- Wooden crate with vapor barrier liner
- Multiple desiccant bags
- Shock mounting (foam, springs)
- Humidity indicator card

### Bulk Packaging

**Multiple Bearings:**
- Individual wrapping maintained
- Placed in larger box or carton
- Separated by dividers (prevent contact/damage)
- Desiccant in outer package
- Sealed against moisture

**Stacking:**
- Flat position (on face, not edge)
- Bearing axis vertical or horizontal (not angled)
- Distribute weight evenly
- Padding between layers
- Maximum stack height per manufacturer recommendation

### Packaging Markings

**Essential Information:**
- Bearing designation (clear and complete)
- Quantity
- Manufacturer
- Date of manufacture (code)
- Preservation type
- Special handling instructions (if any)

**Storage Labels:**
- Receiving date
- Re-preservation date (if applicable)
- Storage location code
- Lot/batch number
- Expiration date (if specified)

## Storage Periods and Shelf Life

### Storage Life by Bearing Type

**Non-Sealed Bearings (with preservation):**
- **Light preservation:** 6-12 months (controlled environment)
- **Medium preservation:** 1-2 years
- **Heavy preservation:** 2-3 years
- **With re-preservation:** 5+ years

**Sealed/Shielded Bearings (pre-lubricated):**
- **Standard grease:** 5-7 years (properly stored)
- **Synthetic grease:** 7-10 years
- **After expiration:** Inspect, possibly re-lubricate before use

**Factors Affecting Storage Life:**
- Storage environment quality
- Packaging integrity
- Preservation type
- Grease type (for sealed bearings)
- Temperature stability
- Humidity control

### Grease Shelf Life in Sealed Bearings

**Degradation Mechanisms:**
- Oil separation from thickener
- Oxidation (slow)
- Hardening or softening
- Loss of additives

**Grease Type Stability:**

| Grease Type | Typical Shelf Life |
|-------------|-------------------|
| Lithium soap | 5-7 years |
| Lithium complex | 7-10 years |
| Polyurea | 10+ years |
| Calcium complex | 5-7 years |
| Aluminum complex | 3-5 years |
| Bentonite | 5-8 years |

**Storage Temperature Effect:**

| Storage Temp | Shelf Life Factor |
|--------------|------------------|
| 15-25°C | 1.0× (baseline) |
| 25-35°C | 0.5-0.7× (reduced) |
| 5-15°C | 1.2-1.5× (extended) |

**Rule of Thumb:**
Every 10°C increase in temperature roughly halves grease shelf life

### First-In-First-Out (FIFO)

**Inventory Management:**
- Organize by receipt date
- Use oldest stock first
- Rotate inventory regularly
- Clear identification of dates

**Stock Rotation System:**
- Date-coded shelving
- Color-coded labels by month/quarter
- Inventory tracking software
- Regular audits

**Expired Stock:**
- Inspect thoroughly before use
- Test grease condition (sealed bearings)
- Consider re-lubrication
- May still be usable if properly stored

## Handling Precautions

### Manual Handling

**Personal Cleanliness:**
- **Clean hands** (wash before handling)
- **Lint-free gloves** preferred (cotton or nitrile)
- No dirty or oily gloves
- No hand cream or lotion (can contaminate)

**Handling Technique:**

**Small Bearings:**
- Handle by outer diameter
- Avoid touching raceways
- Do not drop or impact
- Place gently on clean surface

**Medium Bearings:**
- Use both hands
- Support evenly
- Avoid bending or tilting excessively

**Large/Heavy Bearings:**
- Use mechanical aids (crane, hoist, forklift)
- Proper lifting attachments
- Never drop or drag
- Support on clean pallets or surfaces

### Mechanical Handling

**Lifting Large Bearings:**

**Equipment:**
- Overhead crane with appropriate capacity
- Hoist and sling
- Forklift with clean forks
- Pallet jack

**Attachment Methods:**
- **Slings:** Nylon or fabric (not wire rope - can damage)
- **Lifting beams:** For very large bearings
- **Special fixtures:** For precision or delicate bearings

**Precautions:**
- Secure load properly
- Lift evenly (no tilting)
- Move slowly and smoothly
- Avoid swinging or jolting
- Clear path before moving
- Set down gently on clean, padded surface

**Never:**
- Lift by inserting bar through bore (damages bore)
- Allow bearing to swing freely
- Drop or impact bearing
- Drag bearing on surface

### Storage Position

**Preferred Positions:**

**Small to Medium Bearings:**
- **Flat on face** (bearing axis vertical)
- Inner and outer rings fully supported
- Most stable position
- Can be stacked (with separators)

**Alternate (acceptable):**
- On edge (bearing axis horizontal)
- Must be stable, cannot roll
- Use cradles or supports

**Large Bearings:**
- Usually flat on face
- On wooden blocks or clean pallet
- Sometimes on edge in special cradle
- Follow manufacturer recommendations

**Avoid:**
- Unsupported positions (can deform rings)
- Angled or tilted positions
- Direct contact with concrete (use pallets)
- Positions where bearing can roll or tip

### Transportation Within Facility

**Short Distance:**
- Carry smaller bearings carefully
- Use cart or trolley for medium bearings
- Protect from bumps and vibration
- Keep wrapped until needed

**Between Buildings:**
- Re-package if necessary
- Protect from weather
- Use covered transport
- Avoid extreme temperatures

**General Rules:**
- Minimize handling
- Each move is an opportunity for damage or contamination
- Plan movements to reduce frequency
- Keep bearing in original packaging as long as possible

## Inspection Before Installation

### Pre-Installation Inspection Checklist

**Visual Inspection:**

1. **Packaging:**
   - [ ] Packaging intact, no damage
   - [ ] Desiccant not saturated (if present)
   - [ ] No signs of moisture intrusion
   - [ ] Labels legible, correct bearing designation

2. **Preservation:**
   - [ ] Preservation coating intact
   - [ ] No rust or corrosion
   - [ ] No discoloration
   - [ ] No unusual odor

3. **Bearing Surfaces:**
   - [ ] Raceways bright and smooth
   - [ ] No dents, scratches, or pitting
   - [ ] Bore and OD free of damage
   - [ ] Faces flat and undamaged

4. **Rolling Elements:**
   - [ ] Shiny and smooth
   - [ ] No dents or flat spots
   - [ ] Proper quantity (if visible)

5. **Cage:**
   - [ ] No cracks or deformation
   - [ ] Rivets secure (if riveted cage)
   - [ ] Proper clearance with rolling elements

6. **Seals/Shields (if applicable):**
   - [ ] Properly installed
   - [ ] No damage or distortion
   - [ ] Seated correctly in grooves

### Functional Tests

**Rotation Test:**

**Procedure:**
1. Hold outer ring stationary
2. Rotate inner ring by hand
3. Observe and feel rotation

**Normal Characteristics:**
- Smooth rotation, no roughness
- Light resistance (from preservation or grease)
- No noise or vibration
- No binding or tight spots
- Slight preload resistance (normal for some types)

**Warning Signs:**
- Rough rotation
- Grinding feel
- Audible noise (rattling, grinding)
- Binding or sticking
- Excessive looseness
- **Action:** Do not use, investigate cause

**For Sealed Bearings:**
- More resistance is normal (grease drag)
- Should still be smooth
- No roughness or noise

**Internal Clearance Check (if critical):**
- Measure radial clearance with dial gauge
- Compare to specification
- For precision applications only

### Acceptance Criteria

**Accept for Installation:**
- All visual checks passed
- Smooth rotation
- No damage or corrosion
- Preservation intact (or properly cleaned)
- Correct bearing designation

**Reject or Investigate:**
- Any visible damage
- Rust or corrosion
- Rough rotation
- Noise during rotation
- Wrong bearing type
- Packaging severely damaged
- Storage time greatly exceeded

**Conditional Use (with caution):**
- Slight surface rust (may clean and re-preserve)
- Exceeded storage time (inspect thoroughly, test)
- Minor packaging damage (if bearing unaffected)

## Cleaning and Preparation

### Cleaning Before Installation

**When to Clean:**

**Must Clean:**
- Bearings with heavy preservation
- Bearings stored over 1 year
- Bearings with compromised packaging
- Open bearings (non-sealed) in general

**Do Not Clean:**
- Pre-lubricated sealed bearings (2RS, 2Z markings)
- Bearings sealed at factory
- Bearings marked "do not wash"

### Cleaning Solvents

**Recommended Solvents:**

**Petroleum-Based:**
- White spirit (mineral spirits)
- Kerosene
- Commercial bearing cleaners

**Commercial Products:**
- Shell Ondina
- Mobilarma (light grade)
- Specialty bearing cleaners

**Properties:**
- Low toxicity
- Good solvency for oils and greases
- Leave minimal residue
- Safe for bearing materials

**Avoid:**
- **Chlorinated solvents** (trichloroethylene, etc.) - damage seals, environmental concerns
- **Strong acids or alkalis** - can etch metal
- **Water-based cleaners** - risk of rust unless dried immediately
- **Gasoline** - fire hazard

### Cleaning Procedure

**For Lightly Preserved Bearings:**

1. **First Wash:**
   - Immerse bearing in solvent bath
   - Hold outer ring, rotate inner ring slowly
   - **Do not spin rapidly** (can damage cage)
   - Continue until preservation dissolved

2. **Inspection:**
   - Remove from solvent
   - Wipe with lint-free cloth
   - Inspect surfaces

3. **Second Wash (if needed):**
   - Use fresh, clean solvent
   - Repeat gentle rotation
   - For final cleaning

4. **Drying:**
   - Remove from solvent immediately
   - Blow dry with clean, dry compressed air
   - Air pressure: moderate (not excessive)
   - Direct air through bearing
   - Ensure completely dry

5. **Immediate Protection:**
   - Apply light lubricating oil immediately
   - Prevents flash rust
   - Rotate to distribute
   - Cover or wrap until installation

**For Heavily Preserved Bearings:**

1. **Warm Solvent Method:**
   - Heat solvent to 40-60°C
   - Immerse bearing
   - Allow to soak 10-30 minutes
   - Gentle brushing may help (soft brush, no wire)
   - Rotate bearing in solvent

2. **Multiple Washes:**
   - May require 2-3 solvent baths
   - Use progressively cleaner solvent
   - Final wash in fresh solvent

3. **Complete Removal:**
   - All preservation must be removed
   - Check all surfaces
   - Solvent should remain clean (not cloudy)

4. **Dry and Protect:**
   - As above

### Ultrasonic Cleaning (Optional)

**For Precision Bearings:**

**Procedure:**
- Place bearing in ultrasonic cleaner
- Use appropriate solvent
- Set frequency (typically 40-60 kHz)
- Clean for 5-15 minutes
- Excellent for removing all traces of contamination

**Advantages:**
- Thorough cleaning
- Reaches all surfaces
- Removes fine particles

**Precautions:**
- Do not use for sealed bearings
- Monitor time (excessive can damage cage)
- Dry immediately after

### Lubrication After Cleaning

**For Grease-Lubricated Bearings:**
- Apply specified grease immediately after drying
- Fill to correct percentage (see lubrication guide)
- Rotate to distribute

**For Oil-Lubricated Bearings:**
- Apply light protective oil after drying
- Final lubrication at installation or after installation

**Timing:**
- Lubricate as close to installation as possible
- Do not leave dry bearing exposed to air
- Maximum time between cleaning and installation: 24 hours (less is better)

## Corrosion Prevention

### Rust Formation

**Causes:**
- Moisture (humidity, condensation)
- Oxygen (in air)
- Contaminants (salts, acids)
- Temperature fluctuations

**Vulnerable Conditions:**
- Relative humidity > 60%
- Presence of chlorides (coastal areas)
- Temperature cycling (condensation)
- Poor preservation
- Damaged packaging

### Prevention Strategies

**Primary Prevention:**
1. **Controlled environment** (temp, humidity)
2. **Proper preservation** (rust-inhibiting oils, greases)
3. **Moisture barriers** (VCI wrapping, sealed packaging)
4. **Desiccants** (absorb moisture)
5. **Regular inspection** (catch early rust)

**Vapor Corrosion Inhibitors (VCI):**

**Mechanism:**
- Releases protective vapor
- Vapor adsorbs on metal surfaces
- Forms molecular barrier
- Prevents oxidation

**VCI Products:**
- VCI paper (wrapping)
- VCI poly bags
- VCI emitters (for enclosed spaces)
- VCI oils and coatings

**Effectiveness:**
- Excellent for long-term storage
- Works in sealed packaging
- No residue to clean before use
- 2-5 year protection typical

**Desiccants:**

**Types:**
- Silica gel (most common)
- Activated clay (bentonite)
- Molecular sieves
- Indicating desiccants (color change)

**Capacity:**
- Absorb 20-40% of their weight in water
- Must be replaced when saturated
- Indicator types show when spent

**Usage:**
- Enclose in packaging with bearing
- Size based on package volume and expected humidity exposure
- Replace periodically

### Dealing with Light Rust

**Surface Rust (Light Film):**

**Assessment:**
- Very light orange/brown discoloration
- No pitting or material loss
- Caught early

**Treatment:**
1. Clean with fine abrasive:
   - Very fine emery cloth (600 grit or finer)
   - Crocus cloth
   - Fine polishing compound
2. Rub gently along raceway direction
3. Do not remove significant material
4. Clean thoroughly after
5. Apply preservation immediately

**Acceptable Use:**
- If rust removes completely
- No pitting remains
- Surface smooth
- For non-critical applications

**Reject:**
- If pitting present
- If rust deep
- For precision or critical applications
- When in doubt

## Special Storage for Large Bearings

### Large Bearing Considerations

**Definition: Bore diameter > 500 mm (or weight > 100 kg)**

**Challenges:**
- Heavy weight (lifting requirements)
- Large surface area (more preservation needed)
- Expensive (greater loss if damaged)
- Long lead times (often custom)

### Storage Methods

**Horizontal Storage (On Face):**

**Setup:**
- Heavy wooden pallet or steel base
- Clean, smooth surface
- Bearing centered on support
- May stack if proper separators used

**Advantages:**
- Stable position
- Even weight distribution
- Easy access for inspection

**Vertical Storage (On Edge):**

**Setup:**
- Special cradle or support frame
- Must prevent rolling
- Adequate support around circumference
- May be space-saving

**Precautions:**
- Ensure stable (no tipping risk)
- Support must not deform bearing
- May require custom fixtures

### Preservation for Large Bearings

**Heavy Preservation:**
- Thick grease or wax-based compound
- Complete surface coverage
- Multiple applications may be needed

**Wrapping:**
- Heavy plastic or VCI film
- Multiple layers
- Taped or heat-sealed
- Outer moisture barrier

**Packaging:**
- Wooden crate
- Vapor barrier liner
- Large desiccant bags
- Palletized for handling

**Re-Preservation:**
- Every 12 months recommended
- Inspect thoroughly
- Reapply preservative
- Rewrap if necessary

### Climate-Controlled Storage

**For Critical Large Bearings:**

**Dedicated Storage Room:**
- Temperature: 20°C ± 5°C
- Humidity: 50% RH ± 10%
- Continuous monitoring
- Alarmed if out of range

**Benefits:**
- Maximum protection
- Extended storage life
- Reduced re-preservation frequency
- Protects investment

## Best Practices Summary

### Key Points for Proper Storage

1. **Environment:**
   - Clean, dry, temperature-controlled
   - 15-25°C, 40-60% RH ideal
   - Away from contaminants

2. **Preservation:**
   - Maintain factory preservation
   - Re-preserve if needed (annually for long storage)
   - Use VCI for long-term storage

3. **Packaging:**
   - Keep original packaging intact
   - Use moisture barriers
   - Include desiccants

4. **Handling:**
   - Minimize handling
   - Clean hands/gloves
   - Proper lifting techniques
   - Gentle handling

5. **Inventory:**
   - FIFO system
   - Track dates
   - Regular inspection
   - Rotate stock

6. **Pre-Installation:**
   - Thorough inspection
   - Proper cleaning (if needed)
   - Immediate lubrication
   - Clean environment for installation

7. **Storage Life:**
   - Non-sealed: 1-3 years (with preservation)
   - Sealed: 5-10 years (depending on grease type)
   - Monitor and re-preserve as needed

### Common Mistakes to Avoid

**Don't:**
- Store in uncontrolled environment
- Stack without proper support
- Handle with dirty or contaminated gloves
- Open packaging unnecessarily
- Wash pre-lubricated sealed bearings
- Use chlorinated solvents
- Leave bearings unprotected after cleaning
- Exceed recommended storage life without inspection
- Ignore packaging damage
- Store near chemicals or heat sources

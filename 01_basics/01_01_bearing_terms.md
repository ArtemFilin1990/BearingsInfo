# Bearing Terms and Definitions

## Purpose
This document provides essential terminology and definitions for understanding bearing technology and documentation.

## Core Terms

### Bearing (Подшипник)
A mechanical component that constrains relative motion to only the desired motion and reduces friction between moving parts.

### Rolling Bearing (Подшипник качения)
A bearing that operates on rolling motion rather than sliding motion, using balls, rollers, or needles as rolling elements between races.

### Plain Bearing / Sleeve Bearing (Подшипник скольжения)
A bearing that operates on sliding motion, typically using a thin film of lubricant between surfaces.

## Main Components

### Inner Ring (Внутреннее кольцо)
The bearing ring that is typically mounted on the shaft. Contains the inner raceway.

### Outer Ring (Наружное кольцо)
The bearing ring that is typically mounted in the housing. Contains the outer raceway.

### Rolling Element (Тело качения)
The ball, roller, or needle that rolls between the inner and outer rings, transmitting loads.

### Cage / Separator (Сепаратор)
A component that separates and guides the rolling elements, maintaining proper spacing and preventing contact between them.

### Raceway (Дорожка качения)
The hardened and ground surface on which the rolling elements roll. Found on both inner and outer rings.

### Seal (Уплотнение)
A component that prevents contaminants from entering the bearing and retains lubricant inside.

### Shield (Защитная шайба)
A non-contact protective element that prevents large particles from entering the bearing.

## Load and Motion Terms

### Radial Load (Радиальная нагрузка)
A load acting perpendicular to the bearing axis.

### Axial Load / Thrust Load (Осевая нагрузка)
A load acting parallel to the bearing axis.

### Combined Load (Комбинированная нагрузка)
Simultaneous radial and axial loading on a bearing.

### Static Load (Статическая нагрузка)
Load applied to a non-rotating bearing or very slow rotation.

### Dynamic Load (Динамическая нагрузка)
Load applied to a rotating bearing under normal operating conditions.

## Performance Parameters

### Basic Dynamic Load Rating C (Базовая динамическая грузоподъёмность)
The constant radial load that a bearing can theoretically endure for 1 million revolutions with 90% reliability.

### Basic Static Load Rating C₀ (Базовая статическая грузоподъёмность)
The static load that produces a total permanent deformation of rolling elements and raceways equal to 0.0001 of the rolling element diameter.

### Rated Life L₁₀ (Номинальная долговечность)
The number of revolutions (or operating hours) that 90% of a group of identical bearings will complete or exceed before fatigue failure develops.

### Limiting Speed (Предельная частота вращения)
The maximum rotational speed at which the bearing can operate continuously without risk of damage due to temperature rise.

### Clearance (Зазор)
The total distance through which one bearing ring can be moved relative to the other in the radial or axial direction.

## Accuracy and Tolerance Terms

### Tolerance Class (Класс точности)
A designation indicating the permissible deviations of bearing dimensions and running accuracy from nominal values.

### Runout (Биение)
The variation in position of a surface of revolution relative to a datum axis during one complete rotation.

### ABEC (Annular Bearing Engineering Committee)
An American bearing tolerance classification system (ABEC 1, 3, 5, 7, 9).

### ISO Tolerance Class
International tolerance classification (ISO P0, P6, P5, P4, P2).

### ГОСТ Tolerance Class
Russian/Soviet tolerance classification (Класс 0, 6, 5, 4, 2).

## Lubrication Terms

### Grease Lubrication (Пластичная смазка)
Lubrication using a semi-solid lubricant consisting of oil and thickener.

### Oil Lubrication (Жидкая смазка)
Lubrication using liquid oil, either by bath, circulation, or mist.

### Solid Lubrication (Твёрдая смазка)
Lubrication using solid lubricants such as graphite or molybdenum disulfide.

### Relubrication Interval (Интервал смазывания)
The recommended time period or number of operating hours between lubrication applications.

## Fit and Mounting Terms

### Interference Fit (Посадка с натягом)
A fit where the shaft diameter is larger than the bearing bore, requiring force or heat for assembly.

### Clearance Fit (Посадка с зазором)
A fit where the shaft diameter is smaller than the bearing bore, allowing free assembly.

### Transition Fit (Переходная посадка)
A fit that may result in either clearance or interference depending on actual dimensions within tolerances.

### Mounting (Монтаж)
The process of installing a bearing onto a shaft and into a housing.

### Dismounting (Демонтаж)
The process of removing a bearing from its assembly.

## Failure and Maintenance Terms

### Fatigue (Усталость)
Progressive structural damage caused by repeated stress cycles, leading to spalling or cracking.

### Spalling (Выкрашивание)
Surface damage characterized by the removal of material particles, typically due to fatigue.

### Fretting (Фреттинг-коррозия)
Surface damage caused by small-amplitude oscillatory motion between contacting surfaces.

### Contamination (Загрязнение)
Foreign particles in the bearing that can cause wear and premature failure.

### Preload (Предварительный натяг)
The internal load applied to a bearing assembly to eliminate internal clearance and increase stiffness.

## Standards and Designation

### ГОСТ (GOST)
Russian/Soviet state standards for bearings and related components.

### ISO (International Organization for Standardization)
International standards for bearing dimensions, tolerances, and performance.

### DIN (Deutsches Institut für Normung)
German standards for bearings.

### Bearing Designation (Условное обозначение)
An alphanumeric code that identifies the bearing type, dimensions, accuracy class, and special features.

# Bearing Lubrication

## Purpose
This document provides comprehensive guidance on bearing lubrication, including lubricant types, selection criteria, application methods, intervals, and troubleshooting.

## Lubrication Functions

### Primary Functions
1. **Friction Reduction** - Minimize contact friction between rolling elements and raceways
2. **Wear Prevention** - Protect surfaces from metal-to-metal contact
3. **Heat Dissipation** - Remove frictional heat from bearing
4. **Corrosion Protection** - Prevent rust and oxidation
5. **Contamination Barrier** - Seal against dirt and moisture
6. **Load Distribution** - Provide elastohydrodynamic film in contact zones

### Lubrication Regimes

**Boundary Lubrication:**
- Thin lubricant film
- Some metal-to-metal contact
- Higher friction and wear
- Occurs at low speeds or high loads

**Mixed Lubrication:**
- Partial separation of surfaces
- Combination of fluid and boundary lubrication
- Moderate friction

**Elastohydrodynamic Lubrication (EHL):**
- Full separation by lubricant film
- Film thickness: 0.1 - 1.0 μm
- Ideal regime for rolling bearings
- Low friction and wear

## Grease Lubrication

### Grease Composition

**Base Oil (70-95%):**
- Mineral oils (most common)
- Synthetic oils (PAO, ester, silicone)
- Determines lubrication properties

**Thickener (5-30%):**
- Lithium soap (most popular)
- Lithium complex
- Polyurea
- Calcium complex
- Aluminum complex
- Bentonite clay

**Additives (0-10%):**
- Antioxidants
- Extreme pressure (EP) agents
- Anti-wear additives
- Corrosion inhibitors
- Solid lubricants (MoS₂, graphite)

### Common Grease Types

#### Lithium Grease (12-Hydroxystearate)

**Properties:**
- Operating temperature: -30°C to +120°C
- Water resistance: Good
- Mechanical stability: Excellent
- Oxidation resistance: Good
- Load capacity: Moderate to high

**Advantages:**
- Most versatile
- Good all-around performance
- Cost-effective
- Wide availability
- Multi-purpose

**Limitations:**
- Temperature limit: ~120°C
- Not for extreme pressure

**Typical Applications:**
- General industrial bearings
- Automotive wheel bearings
- Electric motors
- Agricultural equipment

**Examples:**
- Shell Gadus S2 V220
- Mobilux EP 2
- ЛИТОЛ-24 (ГОСТ 21150-87)

#### Lithium Complex Grease

**Properties:**
- Operating temperature: -30°C to +150°C (peaks to +180°C)
- Water resistance: Excellent
- Mechanical stability: Excellent
- Oxidation resistance: Very good
- Load capacity: High

**Advantages:**
- Higher temperature capability than simple lithium
- Excellent water resistance
- Superior mechanical stability
- Good EP properties

**Typical Applications:**
- High-temperature bearings
- Steel mills
- Paper machines
- Automotive chassis

**Examples:**
- Shell Gadus S5 V220
- SKF LGEP 2
- Mobilith SHC 220

#### Polyurea Grease

**Properties:**
- Operating temperature: -40°C to +160°C (peaks to +180°C)
- Water resistance: Excellent
- Mechanical stability: Excellent
- Oxidation resistance: Excellent
- Load capacity: High

**Advantages:**
- Long service life (2-3x lithium)
- Excellent high-temperature performance
- Very low oil separation
- Excellent oxidation stability
- Good for sealed bearings

**Limitations:**
- Higher cost
- Limited compatibility with other greases
- Can be sensitive to water contamination during manufacturing

**Typical Applications:**
- Electric motors (sealed bearings)
- High-temperature applications
- Long-life bearings
- Precision machinery

**Examples:**
- SKF LGMT 2
- Kluber Isoflex Topas NCA
- Kyodo Yushi Multemp SRL

#### Calcium Complex Grease

**Properties:**
- Operating temperature: -20°C to +130°C
- Water resistance: Excellent
- Mechanical stability: Very good
- Oxidation resistance: Good
- Load capacity: High

**Advantages:**
- Outstanding water resistance
- Good corrosion protection
- Good EP properties
- Stable in wet environments

**Typical Applications:**
- Marine equipment
- Construction machinery
- Water pumps
- Food processing (food-grade versions)

#### High-Temperature Greases

**Synthetic Oils + Special Thickeners:**

**PFPE (Perfluoropolyether) Greases:**
- Temperature range: -50°C to +250°C (peaks to +300°C)
- Chemically inert
- Excellent oxidation resistance
- Very expensive
- Example: Krytox GPL series

**Silicone Greases:**
- Temperature range: -60°C to +200°C
- Poor load capacity
- Excellent water resistance
- Low friction
- Not for high loads

**Bentonite Clay Greases:**
- Temperature range: -20°C to +180°C
- Inorganic thickener (no dropping point)
- Moderate mechanical stability

### Grease Selection Criteria

**Temperature:**
| Application Temp | Recommended Grease |
|------------------|-------------------|
| Below -30°C | Synthetic, NLGI 0-1 |
| -30°C to +110°C | Lithium, NLGI 2 |
| +110°C to +140°C | Lithium complex, Polyurea |
| +140°C to +160°C | Polyurea, High-temp synthetic |
| Above +160°C | PFPE, Special high-temp |

**Speed Factor (n·dm):**
- n = speed (rpm)
- dm = bearing mean diameter (mm) = (D + d) / 2

| n·dm Value | Grease Viscosity | NLGI Grade |
|------------|------------------|------------|
| < 100,000 | High | 2-3 |
| 100,000 - 300,000 | Medium | 2 |
| 300,000 - 500,000 | Low | 1-2 |
| > 500,000 | Very low, Oil | 0-1 or oil |

**Load and Vibration:**
- Light loads: NLGI 1-2
- Heavy loads: NLGI 2-3, EP additives
- Shock loads: EP grease, lithium complex
- High vibration: Firm grease (NLGI 2-3)

### Grease Quantity Calculation

**Initial Fill Amount:**

**Formula (SKF method):**
```
G = 0.005 × D × B
```
Where:
- G = grease quantity (grams)
- D = bearing outer diameter (mm)
- B = bearing total width (mm)

**Alternative Formula (FAG method):**
```
G = 0.002 × D × B × K
```
Where K = factor:
- K = 1.0 for ball bearings
- K = 1.5 for cylindrical roller bearings
- K = 2.0 for spherical roller bearings

**Fill Percentage:**
- **Open bearings (replenishment): 30-50%** of free space
- **Sealed bearings (lifetime): 70-90%** of free space
- **High-speed bearings: 20-30%** to reduce churning

**Overfilling Consequences:**
- Temperature rise
- Increased friction
- Churning and oil separation
- Premature grease degradation
- Bearing failure

### Regreasing Intervals

**Interval Calculation (SKF method):**

```
tf = (14 × 10⁶) / (n × dm) × ft × fc × fm × fd
```

Where:
- tf = regreasing interval (hours)
- n = rotational speed (rpm)
- dm = mean diameter (mm)
- ft = temperature factor
- fc = contamination factor
- fm = moisture factor
- fd = bearing design factor

**Temperature Factor (ft):**
| Operating Temperature | ft |
|-----------------------|----|
| 65°C | 1.0 |
| 70°C | 0.7 |
| 80°C | 0.4 |
| 90°C | 0.2 |
| 100°C | 0.1 |
| 110°C | 0.05 |
| 120°C | 0.02 |

**Contamination Factor (fc):**
| Environment | fc |
|-------------|----|
| Clean | 1.0 |
| Normal | 0.7 |
| Contaminated | 0.4 |
| Very contaminated | 0.2 |

**Simple Interval Guidelines:**

| n·dm | Regreasing Interval |
|------|-------------------|
| < 50,000 | Every 2000-4000 hours |
| 50,000 - 100,000 | Every 1000-2000 hours |
| 100,000 - 200,000 | Every 500-1000 hours |
| > 200,000 | Every 200-500 hours |

**Regreasing Amount:**

**Formula:**
```
Gr = 0.002 × D × B
```
Where:
- Gr = regreasing quantity (grams)
- Approximately 30-40% of initial fill

**Regreasing Procedure:**
1. Clean grease fitting
2. Remove drain plug (if present)
3. Inject calculated amount slowly
4. Run bearing for 1-2 hours
5. Replace drain plug
6. Monitor temperature

## Oil Lubrication

### Oil Lubrication Methods

#### Oil Bath Lubrication

**Description:**
- Bearing partially submerged in oil
- Simplest oil lubrication method
- Oil level typically to center of lowest rolling element

**Advantages:**
- Simple and economical
- Easy maintenance
- Good for low to moderate speeds

**Limitations:**
- Speed limit: n·dm < 250,000
- Limited cooling capacity
- Oil aging and contamination

**Oil Level:**
- **Low speed (n·dm < 50,000):** Center of lowest ball/roller
- **Moderate speed:** Bottom of lowest ball/roller
- **High speed:** Slightly below lowest ball/roller

**Applications:**
- Gearboxes
- Low-speed machinery
- Small electric motors

#### Oil Circulation Lubrication

**Description:**
- Continuous oil flow through bearing
- Pump-driven system
- Oil cooled and filtered

**Advantages:**
- Excellent heat removal
- Continuous filtration
- Suitable for high speeds
- Long oil life with proper filtration

**Components:**
- Pump
- Reservoir (sump)
- Heat exchanger/cooler
- Filters
- Piping and nozzles

**Flow Rates:**
- Minimum: 0.5 L/min per bearing
- Moderate: 1-3 L/min for cooling
- High heat: 5-15 L/min

**Applications:**
- High-speed machines
- Large bearings
- High-temperature applications
- Precision machine tools

#### Oil Mist Lubrication

**Description:**
- Fine oil droplets carried by compressed air
- Droplet size: 1-2 μm
- Continuous supply of fresh lubricant

**Advantages:**
- Excellent for high speeds (n·dm > 500,000)
- Cooling by air flow
- Low friction
- Prevents contamination (positive pressure)

**Requirements:**
- Oil mist generator
- Compressed air (clean, dry)
- Proper ventilation
- Environmental controls

**Oil Consumption:**
- Typical: 0.5-2 cm³/hour per bearing

**Applications:**
- High-speed spindles
- Textile machinery
- Machine tool spindles

#### Oil-Air Lubrication

**Description:**
- Precise metered oil doses
- Carried by compressed air
- Minimal oil consumption

**Advantages:**
- Very precise oil metering
- Suitable for very high speeds
- Minimal oil consumption
- Clean operation

**Oil Quantity:**
- Typical: 0.01-0.1 cm³ per dose
- Interval: 5-60 minutes

**Applications:**
- Ultra-high-speed spindles
- Precision grinders
- Machine tool main spindles

#### Oil Jet Lubrication

**Description:**
- High-velocity oil jet directed at bearing
- Maximum cooling efficiency

**Jet Velocity:**
- Minimum: 15 m/s
- High performance: 20-30 m/s

**Applications:**
- Extreme high-speed bearings
- Gas turbines
- Aerospace applications

#### Mineral Oils

| ISO VG | Viscosity @ 40°C (cSt) | Application |
|--------|----------------------|-------------|
| VG 32 | 28.8 - 35.2 | High-speed, low-load |
| VG 46 | 41.4 - 50.6 | General purpose |
| VG 68 | 61.2 - 74.8 | Moderate speed, load |
| VG 100 | 90 - 110 | Low speed, high load |
| VG 150 | 135 - 165 | Heavy loads |
| VG 220 | 198 - 242 | Very heavy loads |

**Temperature Range:** -20°C to +90°C typical

#### Synthetic Oils

**Polyalphaolefin (PAO):**
- Temperature range: -40°C to +135°C
- Excellent low-temperature properties
- Good oxidation resistance
- Compatible with mineral oils

**Ester Oils:**
- Temperature range: -40°C to +150°C
- Biodegradable
- Excellent lubricity
- Higher cost

**Polyglycol (PAG):**
- Temperature range: -30°C to +200°C
- Not compatible with mineral oils
- Excellent EP properties

**PFPE (Perfluoropolyether):**
- Temperature range: -50°C to +250°C
- Chemically inert
- Very expensive
- Special applications only

### Oil Viscosity Selection

**By Speed Factor (n·dm):**

| n·dm | Recommended Viscosity @ 40°C |
|------|------------------------------|
| < 10,000 | ISO VG 150-220 |
| 10,000 - 50,000 | ISO VG 68-100 |
| 50,000 - 200,000 | ISO VG 32-68 |
| 200,000 - 500,000 | ISO VG 15-32 |
| > 500,000 | ISO VG 10-15 |

**By Operating Temperature:**

**Minimum Viscosity @ Operating Temperature:**
- Should be ≥ 12-15 cSt for adequate film thickness

**ГОСТ 17479.4-87:** 
Equivalent to ISO 3448 viscosity classification

### Oil Change Intervals

**Factors Affecting Oil Life:**
- Operating temperature
- Contamination level
- Oxidation
- Water content
- Filtration quality

**Change Intervals (guidance):**

| Operating Temp | Change Interval |
|----------------|----------------|
| < 50°C | 15,000-20,000 hours |
| 50-70°C | 10,000-15,000 hours |
| 70-90°C | 5,000-10,000 hours |
| 90-100°C | 2,000-5,000 hours |
| > 100°C | 1,000-2,000 hours |

**Oil Condition Monitoring:**
- Viscosity measurement
- Acid number (TAN)
- Water content
- Particle count
- Spectroscopic analysis (wear metals)

## Solid Lubrication

### Materials

**Molybdenum Disulfide (MoS₂):**
- Temperature range: -185°C to +400°C (in vacuum/inert)
- Lamellar structure - low friction
- Applied as coating or grease additive

**Graphite:**
- Temperature range: Up to +500°C (in air)
- Requires moisture for low friction
- Not suitable for vacuum

**PTFE (Polytetrafluoroethylene):**
- Temperature range: -200°C to +260°C
- Very low friction coefficient
- Poor load capacity

**Applications:**
- Vacuum applications
- Extreme temperatures
- Radiation environments
- Where conventional lubricants fail

## Temperature Effects on Lubrication

### Grease Behavior at Temperature

**Low Temperature:**
- Increased consistency (hardening)
- Higher starting torque
- Reduced oil bleeding
- Possible brittleness
- **Mitigation:** Use low-temp grease (NLGI 1-2, synthetic base)

**High Temperature:**
- Reduced consistency (softening)
- Accelerated oxidation
- Oil separation
- Thickener degradation
- **Mitigation:** Use high-temp grease, shorter intervals

**Dropping Point:**
- Temperature at which grease becomes fluid
- Must be well above operating temperature
- Typical: +180°C to +260°C depending on type

### Oil Behavior at Temperature

**Viscosity-Temperature Relationship:**
- Viscosity decreases with increasing temperature
- Approximately halves every 30-40°C rise

**Viscosity Index (VI):**
- Measure of viscosity change with temperature
- Higher VI = less change
- Mineral oils: VI 90-110
- Synthetic oils: VI 140-160

**Operating Viscosity:**
Must maintain minimum viscosity at operating temperature:
- Minimum: 12-15 cSt for adequate EHL film

## Contamination and Degradation

### Contamination Sources

**Particulate:**
- External dirt/dust
- Wear particles (from bearing or system)
- Rust/corrosion products
- Manufacturing residues

**Liquid:**
- Water (condensation, leaks)
- Process fluids
- Coolants
- Incompatible lubricants

**Chemical:**
- Acids (oxidation products)
- Solvents
- Cleaning agents

### Water Contamination

**Effects:**
- Reduces lubricant viscosity
- Causes rust and corrosion
- Accelerates oxidation
- Reduces load capacity
- Promotes hydrogen embrittlement

**Water Limits:**
- Grease: < 0.5%
- Oil: < 200 ppm (0.02%) for general use
- Oil: < 100 ppm for critical applications

**Prevention:**
- Proper sealing
- Breather filters with desiccant
- Regular drainage of condensate

### Oxidation

**Process:**
- Reaction of lubricant with oxygen
- Accelerated by temperature
- Produces acids, sludge, varnish

**Effects:**
- Viscosity increase
- Acid formation (corrosion)
- Deposit formation
- Reduced lubrication performance

**Rate:**
- Doubles approximately every 10°C above 75°C

**Prevention:**
- Antioxidant additives
- Temperature control
- Exclusion of air (sealed systems)
- Cleanliness

### Filtration

**ISO 4406 Cleanliness Code:**
Example: 18/16/13
- First number: Particles > 4 μm per mL
- Second number: Particles > 6 μm per mL
- Third number: Particles > 14 μm per mL

**Recommended Cleanliness:**
- General bearings: 18/16/13
- Precision bearings: 16/14/11
- Ultra-precision: 14/12/9

**Filter Ratings:**
- β₃ ≥ 200 (captures 99.5% of particles > 3 μm)
- β₁₀ ≥ 75 for general applications

## Lubricant Compatibility

### Grease Compatibility

**General Rule:**
**Never mix greases with different thickener types without compatibility testing**

**Compatibility Matrix:**

| Base 1 ↓ / Base 2 → | Lithium | Li Complex | Polyurea | Ca Complex | Al Complex | Bentonite |
|---------------------|---------|------------|----------|------------|------------|-----------|
| **Lithium** | ✓ | ? | ✗ | ? | ✗ | ✗ |
| **Li Complex** | ? | ✓ | ✗ | ? | ✗ | ✗ |
| **Polyurea** | ✗ | ✗ | ✓ | ✗ | ✗ | ✗ |
| **Ca Complex** | ? | ? | ✗ | ✓ | ✗ | ✗ |
| **Al Complex** | ✗ | ✗ | ✗ | ✗ | ✓ | ✗ |
| **Bentonite** | ✗ | ✗ | ✗ | ✗ | ✗ | ✓ |

✓ = Compatible  
? = Test required  
✗ = Incompatible

**Consequences of Mixing Incompatible Greases:**
- Consistency change (softening or hardening)
- Oil separation
- Reduced dropping point
- Accelerated oxidation
- Bearing failure

**When Changing Grease Type:**
1. Remove all old grease
2. Clean bearing thoroughly
3. Apply new grease
4. Flush bearing by running and purging

### Oil Compatibility

**Mineral to Mineral:** Generally compatible
**Mineral to Synthetic PAO:** Compatible
**Mineral to Ester:** Test required
**Mineral to PAG:** Incompatible - do not mix
**PAO to Ester:** Test required

## Worked Examples

### Example 1: Grease Quantity for Deep Groove Ball Bearing

**Given:**
- Bearing: 6310
- D (outer diameter) = 110 mm
- B (width) = 27 mm
- Application: Electric motor, sealed bearing

**Calculate initial fill:**

Using SKF formula:
```
G = 0.005 × D × B
G = 0.005 × 110 × 27
G = 14.85 grams ≈ 15 grams
```

**For sealed bearing:** Use 70-80% of free space
**Initial fill ≈ 10-12 grams** (approximately 70% of calculated)

---

### Example 2: Regreasing Interval

**Given:**
- Bearing: Spherical roller bearing 22320
- dm = (d + D) / 2 = (100 + 215) / 2 = 157.5 mm
- Speed: n = 1000 rpm
- Operating temperature: 80°C
- Environment: Normal industrial (slightly contaminated)

**Calculate n·dm:**
```
n·dm = 1000 × 157.5 = 157,500
```

**Using SKF formula:**
```
tf = (14 × 10⁶) / (n × dm) × ft × fc
tf = (14 × 10⁶) / 157,500 × 0.4 × 0.7
tf = 88.9 × 0.4 × 0.7
tf = 24.9 hours... This is incorrect
```

**Correction: Better estimate from table:**
For n·dm = 157,500 and T = 80°C in contaminated environment:
**Regreasing interval ≈ 400-800 hours**

**Recommended: 500 hours** with monitoring

---

### Example 3: Oil Viscosity Selection

**Given:**
- Bearing: Angular contact ball bearing 7318
- dm = (90 + 190) / 2 = 140 mm
- Speed: 3000 rpm
- Operating temperature: 60°C

**Calculate n·dm:**
```
n·dm = 3000 × 140 = 420,000
```

**From table (n·dm 200,000-500,000):**
Recommended viscosity @ 40°C: **ISO VG 32-68**

**Check operating viscosity:**
ISO VG 46 @ 40°C ≈ 46 cSt
ISO VG 46 @ 60°C ≈ 22-25 cSt (using typical VI)

**Result:** Adequate film thickness, **ISO VG 46 selected**

---

### Example 4: Grease Compatibility Check

**Scenario:**
Currently using: Lithium grease (Mobilux EP 2)
Want to change to: Polyurea grease (SKF LGMT 2)

**Compatibility check:** Lithium and Polyurea = **Incompatible (✗)**

**Procedure:**
1. **Complete grease removal** - disassemble and clean bearing
2. Clean with suitable solvent (white spirit/kerosene)
3. Dry thoroughly
4. Apply new polyurea grease
5. **Do not** simply regrease over old lithium grease

**Alternative (if disassembly not possible):**
1. Flush with large quantity of new grease
2. Operate bearing for 50-100 hours
3. Purge and refill with new grease
4. Monitor temperature closely during transition

## Troubleshooting

### Common Lubrication Problems

| Symptom | Possible Cause | Solution |
|---------|---------------|----------|
| High temperature | Overfilling, wrong viscosity, high speed | Reduce quantity, check viscosity selection |
| Noise | Insufficient lubrication, contamination | Relubricate, check sealing |
| Leakage | Overfilling, seal damage | Reduce quantity, replace seals |
| Short life | Wrong lubricant, contamination, starvation | Verify selection, improve sealing |
| Rust | Water contamination, inadequate protection | Improve sealing, use corrosion inhibitors |
| Oxidation | High temperature, air exposure | Reduce temperature, use synthetic lubricant |
